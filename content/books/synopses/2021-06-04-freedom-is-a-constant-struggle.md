Angela Davis collects essays, interviews and speeches in this 2016 book, drawing lines between struggles against state violence in different places and periods. She sets Ferguson beside Palestine, and reads both against the Black Freedom Movement and the campaign against apartheid in South Africa. Black feminism, intersectionality and prison abolition run through the collection, which treats these fights as versions of the same one.
