Arundhati Roy's 2020 collection takes its title from the Urdu word for freedom, the chant of the movement against Indian rule in Kashmir and later of protests against Hindu nationalism across India. The essays turn on what the word means when opposing crowds both use it. Roy also writes about language, about what fiction is for, and about the borders the pandemic rendered meaningless.
