Stephen Hawking's book, first published in 1988, addresses questions in cosmology for a general readership: how the universe began, whether time moves only forward, whether the universe has any boundary, and whether other dimensions exist. It covers subjects including black holes, quarks, antimatter, and the big bang. The book became a bestseller and was later the basis for a 1991 documentary film about Hawking, directed by Errol Morris, which is a biography of Hawking rather than a retelling of the book itself.
