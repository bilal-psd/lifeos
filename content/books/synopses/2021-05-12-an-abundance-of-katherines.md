An Abundance of Katherines is a young adult novel by John Green, released in 2006 and named a finalist for the Michael L. Printz Award. The book follows a math-minded protagonist named Colin Singleton, and includes an appendix by Daniel Biss, a close friend of Green's, explaining the equations Colin develops over the course of the story.
