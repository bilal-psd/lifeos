Looking for Alaska is a 2005 young adult novel by John Green. It follows sixteen-year-old Miles Halter, nicknamed Pudge, through his first year at Culver Creek Preparatory School, where he grows close to roommate Chip Martin, classmate Alaska Young, and their friend Takumi Hikohito. A fatal car crash disrupts the group, and Miles spends the rest of the novel searching for the answers left behind. It won an ALA Best Book for Young Adults award.
