Shoba Narayan spent close to twenty years in Bangalore before writing these essays about what makes the city different from other Indian metros. She covers Karnataka food, the local wine crowd, the character of neighbourhoods like Shivaji Nagar, efforts to protect old buildings, and where to find birds and green space. The collection came out in 2023 and is aimed mostly at newcomers and visitors.
