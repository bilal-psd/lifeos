Pierce Brown's 2014 novel is set on a future Mars organised by colour. Darrow is a Red, the lowest caste, and spends his life mining below the surface in the belief that he is making the planet liveable for the generations after him. He then learns the surface has been habitable for a long time already. What follows is his attempt to pass as a Gold and get inside the ruling class.
