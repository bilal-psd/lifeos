Revolution 2020, published in 2011, follows childhood friends Gopal, Raghav, and Aarti in Varanasi as each tries to build a life within a system that rewards corruption. Gopal accepts the system's terms while Raghav fights it, and both are drawn to Aarti. Chetan Bhagat has said the novel grew out of his view of how private coaching institutes exploit families chasing engineering college admission, and he set it in Varanasi for what he called the city's contrast between old character and modern aspiration.
