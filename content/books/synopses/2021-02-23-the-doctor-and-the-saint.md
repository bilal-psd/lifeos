Arundhati Roy examines persistent inequality in India through a sustained critique of Mahatma Gandhi's positions on race, caste, and imperialism. Haymarket Books published the work in 2017. Its title reflects the debate at its center: B. R. Ambedkar appears as "the Doctor" and Gandhi as "the Saint," a framing Roy uses to lay out their opposing views on caste in Indian society.
