Anne Frank kept this diary during the two years she spent hidden with her family in Amsterdam under Nazi occupation. The family was discovered in 1944, and she died of typhus at Bergen-Belsen in 1945. Miep Gies and Bep Voskuijl recovered the papers and gave them to her father Otto, the only member of the household to survive. It has since been published in more than seventy languages.
