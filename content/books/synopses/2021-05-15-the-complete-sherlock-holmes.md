This is the complete Sherlock Holmes canon: the four novels and fifty-six short stories Arthur Conan Doyle wrote about the detective and his companion Dr Watson. Collected in one volume, they run from the earliest novel through the later short-story sequences. The word canon marks Doyle's own writing off from the very large body of Holmes stories other authors have written since.
