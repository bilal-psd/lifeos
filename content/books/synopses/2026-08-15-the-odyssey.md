Homer's epic poem follows Odysseus, king of Ithaca, as he tries to sail home after the ten-year Trojan War. The return takes another ten years and costs him his entire crew. At home he is presumed dead, and his wife Penelope and son Telemachus are left to deal with the suitors who have moved in. It runs to twenty-four books and is among the oldest surviving works of Greek literature.
