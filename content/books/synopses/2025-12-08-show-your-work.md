Austin Kleon's 2014 book is about being found. It follows Steal Like an Artist and argues that sharing your process matters more than raw talent. Kleon sets out ten rules, among them sharing something small every day and sticking around long enough to be noticed. He also covers the etiquette of it, from crediting other people's work to knowing the point where sharing turns into oversharing.
