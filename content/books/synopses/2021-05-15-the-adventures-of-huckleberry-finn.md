Mark Twain's novel follows Huckleberry Finn, a boy who fakes his own death to escape his abusive father and sets off down the Mississippi River, where he is joined by Jim, a man escaping slavery. Twain introduced Huck as a supporting character in The Adventures of Tom Sawyer in 1876 before making him the narrator of his own book in 1884, when Huck is described as thirteen to fourteen years old.
