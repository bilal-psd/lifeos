And Then There Were None is a mystery novel by Agatha Christie, first published in 1939. Ten strangers are summoned to a private island off the coast of Devon by a wealthy, eccentric host who never appears. Cut off from the mainland with no sign of the man who invited them, the guests discover they share one thing in common as the story unfolds around their isolation and the mounting suspicion between them.
