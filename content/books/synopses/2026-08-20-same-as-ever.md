Same as Ever, published on November 7, 2023, is Morgan Housel's follow up to The Psychology of Money. Where most investment thinking works from predictions about an uncertain future, Housel instead collects stories and examples about things in human behavior that rarely change. He argues that this focus on the constant, rather than the unpredictable, can help readers plan for large events without needing to guess exactly how the future will unfold.
