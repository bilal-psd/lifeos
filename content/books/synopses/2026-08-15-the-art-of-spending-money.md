Morgan Housel's 2025 book turns from making money to spending it. He argues there is no single correct way to spend, and looks at what actually drives the decisions: making up for a poor childhood, justifying a job you dislike, or buying status. His advice is to experiment until you find what genuinely suits you, keep your finances separate from your sense of identity, and spend in a way you will not regret later.
