One Indian Girl, published in 2016, is narrated by Radhika Mehta, a senior employee in the distressed debt group at Goldman Sachs who is getting married the week the story opens. It was Chetan Bhagat's first novel written in a female voice, and it follows the personal and professional pressures Radhika faces inside the finance industry as her wedding approaches.
