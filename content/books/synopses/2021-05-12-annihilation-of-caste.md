B. R. Ambedkar wrote this speech in 1936 for an anti-caste conference in Lahore. The organisers read it in advance, judged it too long and too controversial, and withdrew his invitation. Ambedkar published it himself. It was translated into several Indian languages, spread far beyond the audience it was written for, and has been read since as an argument for abolishing caste rather than reforming it.
