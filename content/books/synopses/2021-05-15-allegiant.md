Allegiant, published in 2013, completes Veronica Roth's Divergent trilogy. The faction based society Tris Prior once believed in has broken apart under violence and betrayal, so when she and Tobias get the chance to see what lies beyond the fence surrounding their city, they take it. Instead of the simpler life they hoped for, Tris finds a new reality that upends what she thought she knew, and she must face fresh choices about courage, loyalty, sacrifice, and love.
