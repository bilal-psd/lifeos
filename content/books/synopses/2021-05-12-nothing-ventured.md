Nothing Ventured opens Jeffrey Archer's William Warwick series. Against his father's wishes, William turns down a career at the bar to join London's Metropolitan Police, working from his first months on the beat to a post in Scotland Yard's Art and Antiques squad. Investigating the theft of a Rembrandt from the Fitzmolean Museum, he falls for Beth Rainsford, a museum research assistant with a secret of her own, while collector Miles Faulkner works to stay ahead of him.
