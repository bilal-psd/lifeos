Digital Fortress is a techno-thriller novel by Dan Brown, first published by St. Martin's Press in 1998. The story centers on government surveillance of electronically stored personal information and considers the civil liberties and ethical questions raised by that kind of technology. This particular edition of the novel was issued in 2004.
