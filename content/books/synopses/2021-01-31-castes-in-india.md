This is B. R. Ambedkar's paper presenting an account of how caste developed in India. He first read it at an anthropological seminar in New York in 1916, and it was published in Indian Antiquary the following year. Ambedkar argues that caste emerged after Brahmins adopted strict endogamy within their own group, prompting other communities to copy the practice in order to imitate that self-declared elite. This edition, first published in 1945, is a reprint.
