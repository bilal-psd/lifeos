Five Point Someone: What Not to Do at IIT, published in 2004, is Chetan Bhagat's debut novel. Set at IIT in the early 1990s, it follows student Hari and his friends Ryan and Alok as they try to hold onto their grades, their friendships, and a life outside their studies. The novel has sold more than a million copies worldwide and was adapted into two films, 3 Idiots and Nanban, and a stage play by the theatre company Evam.
