2 States: The Story of My Marriage, published in 2009, follows a couple from two different Indian states who must win their parents' approval to marry. Chetan Bhagat wrote it after leaving his job as an investment banker. It is his fourth novel, following Five Point Someone, One Night at the Call Centre, and The Three Mistakes of My Life.
