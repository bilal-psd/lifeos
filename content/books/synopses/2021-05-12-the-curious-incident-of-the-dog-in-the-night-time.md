Mark Haddon's 2003 novel is narrated by Christopher John Francis Boone, a fifteen-year-old in Swindon, Wiltshire, described as a mathematician with behavioural difficulties. Christopher sets out to solve the killing of a neighbor's dog, a search that leads him to uncover truths about his own family. He knows every country and capital and every prime number up to 7,057, and cannot bear to be touched. The book won the Whitbread Book of the Year and the Commonwealth Writers' Prize for Best First Book.
