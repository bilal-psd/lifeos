John Green and David Levithan co-write this novel about two teenagers who share the name Will Grayson and meet by chance one night in Chicago. Green wrote the chapters for the heterosexual Will Grayson, rendered in capital letters, while Levithan wrote the alternating chapters for the gay will grayson, written in lowercase. Published in April 2010, the book debuted on the New York Times children's best-seller list, where it stayed for three weeks, becoming the first LGBT-themed young adult novel to reach that list.
