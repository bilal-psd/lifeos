Origin is Dan Brown's fifth novel featuring Harvard symbologist Robert Langdon. Langdon travels to the Guggenheim Museum in Bilbao for an event hosted by Edmond Kirsch, a former student turned technology entrepreneur who intends to reveal a discovery he claims will upend the fundamentals of human existence. When the presentation is violently interrupted before Kirsch's findings can be shown, Langdon and the museum's director, Ambra Vidal, flee to Barcelona to track down a password that will unlock the discovery.
