This is J.K. Rowling's debut novel and the first book in the Harry Potter series. On his eleventh birthday, Harry Potter learns he is a wizard with a place at Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry. Alongside Ron Weasley and Hermione Granger, he begins uncovering a mystery involving the dark wizard Lord Voldemort. Bloomsbury published the novel in the United Kingdom in June 1997 as Harry Potter and the Philosopher's Stone; a Scholastic edition followed in the US as the Sorcerer's Stone.
