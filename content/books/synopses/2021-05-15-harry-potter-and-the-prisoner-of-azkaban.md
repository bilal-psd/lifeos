In J.K. Rowling's third Harry Potter novel, Harry spends his third year at Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry investigating Sirius Black, an escaped prisoner believed to be an ally of Lord Voldemort and connected to the deaths of Harry's parents. Ron Weasley and Hermione Granger help him piece the mystery together. Bloomsbury published the book in the United Kingdom on 8 July 1999, where it sold 64,000 copies in its first three days. It won the 1999 Whitbread Children's Book Award among other prizes.
