Yvonne Woon's 2010 novel opens on Renée Winters's sixteenth birthday, when she is still an ordinary teenager with a best friend and a new boyfriend. That changes when she finds her parents dead in the Redwood Forest in what looks like a double murder. After the funeral, her wealthy grandfather sends her to Gottfried Academy, a remote and secretive boarding school in Maine, where she begins her studies among its unusual curriculum and students.
