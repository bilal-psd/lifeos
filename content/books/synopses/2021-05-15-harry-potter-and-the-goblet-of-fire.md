J.K. Rowling's fourth Harry Potter novel follows Harry through his fourth year at Hogwarts, where his name is unexpectedly entered into the Triwizard Tournament, a competition against two visiting schools that he is then forced to compete in. He also hopes, in vain, for an ordinary year. Bloomsbury and Scholastic released the novel simultaneously in the United Kingdom and United States on 8 July 2000, the series' first same-day release. It won the 2001 Hugo Award, the only Harry Potter novel to do so.
