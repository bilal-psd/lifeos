Half Girlfriend, published in 2014, is Chetan Bhagat's sixth novel. Set across rural Bihar, New Delhi, Patna, and New York, it follows Madhav, a Bihari boy who struggles with English, as he falls for Riya, a Delhi girl who agrees to become his half girlfriend rather than commit to a full relationship. The book was later published in Hindi and Gujarati translations.
