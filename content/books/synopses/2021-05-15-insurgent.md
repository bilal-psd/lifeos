Veronica Roth's 2012 novel is the second book in the Divergent trilogy, and it returns to a ruined Chicago divided into factions. Tris Prior is dealing with the aftermath of the first book while the tension between those factions and their competing beliefs hardens into open war. Roth keeps the focus on what Tris owes the people she is trying to protect, and on questions of grief and loyalty.
