Suzanne Collins's novel is set in a future version of North America, where the ruling Capitol forces the twelve districts of Panem to send two young tributes each year into a televised fight to the death. When her younger sister is chosen, sixteen-year-old Katniss Everdeen volunteers to take her place. What follows is the arena, and what surviving it asks of her.
