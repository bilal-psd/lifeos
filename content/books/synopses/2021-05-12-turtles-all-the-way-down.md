Turtles All the Way Down is John Green's fifth solo novel. Aza Holmes is not especially interested in the disappearance of fugitive billionaire Russell Pickett, but her friend Daisy wants the hundred thousand dollar reward, so together they approach Pickett's son, Davis. As Aza tries to be a good daughter, friend, and student, she also has to manage the tightening spiral of her own thoughts. The novel debuted at number one on the New York Times young adult bestseller list.
