The Subtle Art of Not Giving a F*ck is a 2016 nonfiction self-help book by American writer Mark Manson. It argues that struggle is what gives life meaning, and pushes back against a genre of self-help writing that Manson describes as offering unearned positivity instead of practical guidance. The book appeared on bestseller lists at the New York Times and the Globe and Mail.
