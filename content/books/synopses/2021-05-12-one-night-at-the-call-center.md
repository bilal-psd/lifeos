One Night at the Call Center, first published in 2005, follows six coworkers, Shyam, Priyanka, Esha, Vroom, Radhika, and a man known only as Military Uncle, through a single overnight shift at a call center in Gurgaon, Haryana. Each carries a private crisis: a breakup, an arranged marriage, a stalled modeling ambition, a compromised ideal, a husband's affair. On this particular night, Thanksgiving in the United States, a single call changes what happens to all of them. It was Chetan Bhagat's second best selling novel.
