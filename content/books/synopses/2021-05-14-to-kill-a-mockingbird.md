Harper Lee's 1960 novel is set in the American South and loosely draws on her own childhood, including an event that took place near her hometown of Monroeville, Alabama, in 1936, when she was ten. The book won the Pulitzer Prize the year after its release and is widely assigned in American middle and high schools. Atticus Finch, the narrator's father, has become a reference point for readers thinking about integrity in the legal profession.
