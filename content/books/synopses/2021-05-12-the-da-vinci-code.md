Dan Brown's 2003 thriller opens with a murder in the Louvre and a body left ringed by codes. Robert Langdon, a symbologist, and Sophie Neveu, a cryptologist, follow the trail into a dispute between the Priory of Sion and Opus Dei. At the centre of it is the claim that Jesus and Mary Magdalene had a child whose bloodline survived. Langdon had already appeared in Angels and Demons.
