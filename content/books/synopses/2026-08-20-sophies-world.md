Jostein Gaarder's 1991 Norwegian novel introduces Sophie Amundsen, a teenager who starts receiving letters from a philosopher she has never met. They open with two questions: who are you, and where does the world come from. What follows is a course in the history of Western philosophy folded inside a novel. It sold heavily in Norway and won the Deutscher Jugendliteraturpreis in 1994.
