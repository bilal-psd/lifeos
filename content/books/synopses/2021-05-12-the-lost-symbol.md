The Lost Symbol is Dan Brown's third novel to feature Harvard symbologist Robert Langdon. Summoned to the United States Capitol Building, Langdon discovers an object encoded with five symbols that draws him into a hidden world of Masonic history. When his mentor Peter Solomon, a Freemason and philanthropist, is kidnapped, Langdon must follow the clues to save him. It had a first printing of 6.5 million copies and sold a million copies in its first day.
