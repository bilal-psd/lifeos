City of Bones opens Cassandra Clare's Mortal Instruments series. A night out with her friend Simon turns into a nightmare for fifteen year old Clary Fray when she witnesses a murder committed by teenagers no one else can see. After a monster attacks her mother, one of them, Jace, rescues her and identifies himself as a Shadowhunter who fights demons. Clary, who should not be able to see it, joins Jace to search for her missing mother.
