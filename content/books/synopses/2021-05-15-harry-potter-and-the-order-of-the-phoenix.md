J.K. Rowling's fifth and longest Harry Potter novel covers Harry's fifth year at Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry. Lord Voldemort has returned in secret, the Ministry of Magic denies it and obstructs the school, and Harry faces his O.W.L. examinations amid the tension. It sold five million copies in its first day and won the American Library Association's Best Book Award for Young Adults.
