Bring Up the Bodies is Hilary Mantel's second novel in her trilogy about Thomas Cromwell's rise at the court of Henry VIII. It covers the downfall of Anne Boleyn, who has failed to produce a son and alienated the king's allies. Following the death of the discarded Katherine, Anne is left exposed to gossip and conspiracy, and Cromwell moves against her over a three-week trial for adultery and treason. The novel won the Booker Prize.
