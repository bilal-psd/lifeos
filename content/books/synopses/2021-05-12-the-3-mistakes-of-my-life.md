The 3 Mistakes of My Life, published in May 2008 with an initial print run of 420,000 copies, is Chetan Bhagat's third novel. Set in Ahmedabad beginning in late 2000, it follows Govind, who dreams of running his own business, and his friends Ishaan and Omi, who open a cricket shop together. Govind also tutors Ishaan's sister, Vidya, and starts to develop feelings for her, while the three navigate religious politics, an earthquake, and riots in the city.
