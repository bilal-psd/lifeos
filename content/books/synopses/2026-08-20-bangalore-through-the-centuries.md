M. Fazlul Hasan worked for the city corporation and published this history of Bangalore in 1970. It opens with Kempegowda's forts and runs through the Mysore wars, the reigns of Hyder Ali and Tipu Sultan, and the British move of their garrison from Srirangapatna to the Bangalore Cantonment. It closes on the city of the 1970s and the problems Hasan expected it to face: water supply, population and growth.
