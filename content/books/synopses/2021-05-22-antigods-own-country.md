A.V. Sakthidharan reads Kerala's history against the version usually told about it. He starts from Mahabali, the asura king whose defeat Onam commemorates each year, and treats that story as a record of what happened to the region's first peoples under Aryan domination. From there he traces the origins of non-Aryan deities such as Mutthappan and Ayyappan, and sets Adivasi, Dravidian, Buddhist and Dalit traditions against an incoming brahminism.
