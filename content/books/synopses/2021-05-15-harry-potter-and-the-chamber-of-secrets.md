J.K. Rowling's second Harry Potter novel sends Harry back to Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry for his second year. A house-elf named Dobby warns him not to return, and his friend Ron Weasley rescues him from his aunt and uncle's house in a flying car. At school, several students are attacked and left magically frozen, and Harry, Ron, and Hermione Granger investigate. Bloomsbury published the novel in the United Kingdom in 1998, followed by a Scholastic edition in the United States in 1999.
