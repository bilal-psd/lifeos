Robert M. Pirsig's 1974 book blends fiction and autobiography around a father and son's summer motorcycle trip through the American Northwest, and it introduces Pirsig's concept of Quality. Its title plays on Eugen Herrigel's 1948 book Zen in the Art of Archery, though Pirsig notes it has little to do with either Zen Buddhist practice or motorcycle repair. Publishers rejected the manuscript 121 times before one accepted it, expecting no profit from it. Instead it sold 50,000 copies within its first three months.
