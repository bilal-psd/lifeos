Cecelia Ahern's third novel, published in 2005, is set in a small Irish town. Elizabeth Egan has spent her life as the serious one, left to raise her sister and later her nephew Luke after their mother walked out. Luke talks constantly about a friend called Ivan whom nobody else can see. Then Elizabeth starts seeing him too, because Ivan appears only to people who need a friend.
