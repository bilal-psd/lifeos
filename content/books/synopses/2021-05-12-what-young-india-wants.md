What Young India Wants is a 2012 book by Chetan Bhagat that raises pointed questions about the state of the country and argues for solutions toward a more prosperous India. Bhagat asks why students regularly take their own lives, why corruption persists in Indian public life, why political parties rarely cooperate, and whether individual votes make any real difference. The book presents his answers to those questions alongside proposals for change.
