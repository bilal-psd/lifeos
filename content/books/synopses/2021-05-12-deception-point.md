Dan Brown's third novel, published in 2001, follows Rachel Sexton, a White House intelligence analyst sent to verify a NASA discovery. A meteorite has been found that appears to carry proof of extraterrestrial life. The timing is political. The find would help the sitting president, who employs her, and damage the campaign of her estranged father, a senator running against him.
