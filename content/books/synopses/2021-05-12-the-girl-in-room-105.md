The Girl in Room 105, Chetan Bhagat's eighth novel and tenth book overall, opens with its narrator still drinking and messaging his ex-girlfriend Zara four years after their breakup. When Zara messages him on the eve of her birthday and invites him to her hostel, room 105, he goes, and what he finds there sets him on a mission for justice. The book became a bestseller on pre-orders alone and addresses stereotypes and political issues in India.
