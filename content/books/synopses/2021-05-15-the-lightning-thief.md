Rick Riordan's 2005 novel is the first installment in the Percy Jackson and the Olympians series and his first children's book, an action-adventure grounded in Greek mythology written for middle-grade readers. It was recognized among the best children's books of its year. The story later reached the screen twice, as a 2010 film and as the first season of a Disney+ series that ran from December 2023 to January 2024.
