Life Eternal, published in 2012, is Yvonne Woon's sequel to Dead Beautiful. Renée Winters has changed since being brought back to life by a kiss from her soul mate, the undead Dante Berlin, and doctors cannot explain what has happened to her. With Dante's second life running out, Renée learns of the Nine Sisters, scholars said to have found a way to cheat death, and begins to suspect they are tied to the strange visions that recur in her dreams.
