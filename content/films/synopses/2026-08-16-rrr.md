S.S. Rajamouli directs this 2022 Telugu-language period action film, co-written with V. Vijayendra Prasad. N.T. Rama Rao Jr. and Ram Charan play fictionalized versions of the real revolutionaries Komaram Bheem and Alluri Sitarama Raju, imagined meeting in 1920s Delhi before their historical fight against British rule began. The title stands for Rise, Roar, Revolt. Ajay Devgn, Alia Bhatt and Shriya Saran round out the cast, with the film marking the Telugu debuts of several of its non-Telugu stars.
