Richard Marquand directs this 1983 installment, from a screenplay by Lawrence Kasdan and George Lucas. It closes the original Star Wars trilogy as the sixth chronological chapter of the Skywalker Saga. Luke Skywalker opens the film leading a mission to free Han Solo from Jabba the Hutt, then joins the Rebel Alliance as it moves against a second Death Star built by the Emperor. Luke also tries to turn his father, Darth Vader, from the dark side.
