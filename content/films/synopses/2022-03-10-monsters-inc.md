Monsters, Inc. (2001) is set at a factory that generates power for its world by having monsters scare human children through bedroom-closet doors. Sulley and Mike Wazowski are its top scare team until a toddler named Boo slips through into their city, Monstropolis, a place that believes children are toxic. Pete Docter directed the film for Pixar, and the plot follows Sulley and Mike's attempt to hide Boo and return her home undetected.
