M. Night Shyamalan writes and directs this psychological thriller starring Bruce Willis as a child psychologist treating a nine-year-old boy, played by Haley Joel Osment, who claims he can see and talk to the dead. Released in August 1999, the film received six Academy Award nominations, including Best Picture and Best Director, and established Shyamalan's reputation for twist endings. It grossed over $672 million worldwide, making it the second-highest-grossing film of 1999 and, to date, Shyamalan's highest-grossing film.
