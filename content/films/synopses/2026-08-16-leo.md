Lokesh Kanagaraj directs this Tamil action thriller, the third film in his linked Lokesh Cinematic Universe. Vijay plays Parthiban, a mild-mannered cafe owner in Himachal Pradesh whose quiet life is upended when gangsters Antony and Harold Das insist he is Antony's long-lost son, Leo Das. The film drew mixed reviews, praised for Vijay's performance and action sequences but criticized for its writing, while becoming the third highest-grossing Tamil film of all time.
