Free Guy (2021) stars Ryan Reynolds as a bank teller who discovers he is actually a non-player character inside a massively multiplayer online video game. Shawn Levy directed the film from a screenplay by Matt Lieberman and Zak Penn. Once he realizes what he is, Reynolds's character teams up with one of the game's human players to stop the game from being shut down. Jodie Comer, Lil Rel Howery, Joe Keery and Taika Waititi round out the supporting cast.
