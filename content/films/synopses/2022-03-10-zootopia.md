Judy Hopps, the first rabbit on the Zootopia police force, takes on her first real case to prove herself in this 2016 Walt Disney Animation film directed by Byron Howard and Rich Moore. Teaming up reluctantly with con-artist fox Nick Wilde, Judy uncovers a conspiracy tied to a string of predator disappearances across the city. Ginnifer Goodwin and Jason Bateman voice Hopps and Wilde. The film premiered at the Brussels International Animation Film Festival before its March 2016 US release.
