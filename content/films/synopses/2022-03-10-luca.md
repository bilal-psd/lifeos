Luca (2021) is set on the Italian Riviera in the 1950s, where a sea monster boy named Luca can pass as human once he's out of the water. Directed by Enrico Casarosa for Pixar, the film follows Luca and his friend Alberto as they explore the town of Portorosso alongside a local girl named Giulia, hiding what they really are while chasing an ordinary summer. Casarosa based the setting on his own childhood in Genoa, Italy.
