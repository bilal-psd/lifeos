Tommy O'Haver directs and co-writes, with Irene Turner, this biographical drama about Madalyn Murray O'Hair, an atheist activist known for her outspoken public campaigns and for her disappearance and death, which the film follows from her rise to prominence to her end. Melissa Leo stars as O'Hair. The film premiered at South by Southwest in March 2017 and was released on Netflix ten days later, on March 24.
