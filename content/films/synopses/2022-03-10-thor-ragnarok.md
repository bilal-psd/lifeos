Taika Waititi directs this 2017 film, the seventeenth in the Marvel Cinematic Universe and sequel to Thor: The Dark World. Imprisoned on the far side of the universe, Thor must escape the planet Sakaar in time to stop Ragnarok, the destruction of Asgard, at the hands of the new threat Hela. Chris Hemsworth stars alongside Tom Hiddleston, Cate Blanchett, and Mark Ruffalo, who returns as Bruce Banner in a subplot adapted from the comic storyline Planet Hulk.
