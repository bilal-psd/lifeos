James Gunn directs this 2014 film, the tenth in the Marvel Cinematic Universe, from a script he wrote with Nicole Perlman. Twenty-six years after being abducted from Earth, Peter Quill becomes the target of a manhunt after stealing an orb sought by the warlord Ronan the Accuser, and falls in with a group of criminals on the run. Chris Pratt stars alongside Zoe Saldana, Dave Bautista, and Bradley Cooper. The soundtrack draws on pop songs from the 1960s and 1970s.
