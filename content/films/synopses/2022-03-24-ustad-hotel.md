Ustad Hotel, directed by Anwar Rasheed and released in 2012, follows Faizal, played by Dulquer Salmaan, who trained as a chef in Switzerland against his father's wishes. After his father takes away his passport to stop him from taking a job in the UK, Faizal ends up cooking at a small restaurant in Kozhikode run by his grandfather, Karim, played by Thilakan. The Malayalam film won three National Film Awards, including Best Popular Film.
