Susan Johnson directs this teen romantic comedy, adapted by Sofia Alvarez from Jenny Han's 2014 novel. Lana Condor plays Lara Jean, whose private letters to every boy she has ever had feelings for are secretly mailed out, throwing her love life into chaos. Noah Centineo, Janel Parrish, and John Corbett round out the cast. Released by Netflix in August 2018, the film became the first installment in a series that continued with two sequels and the spin-off XO, Kitty.
