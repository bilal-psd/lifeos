Farah Khan writes and directs this Hindi film starring Shah Rukh Khan as Om Prakash Makhija, an obscure actor in 1970s Bombay in love with the superstar Shanti. After Om dies in a suspicious fire that also kills Shanti, he is reincarnated thirty years later as the successful actor Om Kapoor and sets out, with the help of Shanti's lookalike, to find who started the fire. Deepika Padukone co-stars. The film became the highest-grossing Hindi film of 2007.
