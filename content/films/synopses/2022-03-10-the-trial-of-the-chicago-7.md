The Trial of the Chicago 7 (2020) follows the Chicago Seven, a group of anti-Vietnam War protesters charged with conspiracy after clashes with police at the 1968 Democratic National Convention. Aaron Sorkin wrote and directed the film, having first written the screenplay in 2007 with Steven Spielberg originally attached to direct. The ensemble cast includes Sacha Baron Cohen, Eddie Redmayne, Yahya Abdul-Mateen II, Mark Rylance, Frank Langella and Michael Keaton as the group stands trial for inciting riots.
