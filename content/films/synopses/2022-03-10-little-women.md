Greta Gerwig writes and directs this seventh film adaptation of Louisa May Alcott's 1868 novel, following the March sisters, Meg, Jo, Beth, and Amy, as they come of age in Concord, Massachusetts, after the Civil War. Saoirse Ronan stars as Jo, with Timothee Chalamet, Laura Dern, and Meryl Streep in supporting roles. The film grossed $218 million worldwide, earning six Academy Award nominations, including Best Picture, and winning for Best Costume Design.
