Martin Scorsese directs this 2019 film, adapted by Steven Zaillian from Charles Brandt's 2004 book. Robert De Niro plays Frank Sheeran, an Irish-American truck driver in 1950s Pennsylvania who becomes close to mobster Russell Bufalino, played by Joe Pesci. Bufalino sends Sheeran to Chicago to assist union leader Jimmy Hoffa, played by Al Pacino, beginning a friendship between the two men that lasts nearly twenty years.
