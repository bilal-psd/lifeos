Blade Runner 2049 (2017) is set three decades after the original Blade Runner and follows K, an LAPD officer played by Ryan Gosling, who uncovers a secret capable of destabilizing what remains of society. Denis Villeneuve directed the film from a screenplay by Hampton Fancher and Michael Green. K's discovery sends him searching for Rick Deckard, played again by Harrison Ford, who has been missing for thirty years. Edward James Olmos also reprises his role from the 1982 film.
