Jon Watts directs this 2021 film, the 27th entry in the Marvel Cinematic Universe and the third installment in its Spider-Man series. After Spider-Man's identity becomes public, Peter Parker, played by Tom Holland, asks Doctor Strange, played by Benedict Cumberbatch, to cast a spell restoring his secrecy. The spell goes wrong, and Parker must discover what it truly means to be Spider-Man as the stakes rise around him. Chris McKenna and Erik Sommers wrote the screenplay.
