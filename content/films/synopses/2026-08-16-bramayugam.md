Rahul Sadasivan writes and directs this 2024 Malayalam period horror film, shot in black and white. A wandering folk singer in seventeenth-century Kerala takes shelter in a mansion, where an enigmatic cook and a controlling master draw him into a chain of events that changes his life. Mammootty stars alongside Arjun Ashokan and Sidharth Bharathan. He won a National Film Award for Best Actor for the role, and the film later screened at the Academy Museum of Motion Pictures.
