North 24 Kaatham, written and directed by Anil Radhakrishnan Menon in his debut and released in 2013, follows Harikrishnan, a software professional with obsessive compulsive disorder, played by Fahadh Faasil. A statewide shutdown strands him at Paravur railway station on his way to a conference in Trivandrum, where he spends the night with a veteran politician, Gopalan, and a social worker, Narayani. The Malayalam film won the National Film Award for Best Feature Film in Malayalam.
