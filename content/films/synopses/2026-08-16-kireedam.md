Sibi Malayil directs this 1989 Malayalam-language drama, written by A.K. Lohithadas. Mohanlal plays Sethumadhavan, a young man who wants to become a police officer to fulfill his father's wishes. His path changes after he intervenes in a fight to save his father's life, provoking a criminal he then cannot escape. Mohanlal received a National Film Award Special Mention for the role, and Lohithadas won a Kerala Film Critics Award. Johnson composed the music.
