Curry Barker writes, directs, and edits this supernatural horror film, his second feature after Milk & Serial. Michael Johnston plays Bear, a music store employee who buys a toy called the One Wish Willow and uses it to make his friend Nikki, played by Inde Navarrette, fall in love with him. The wish works, but her feelings curdle into obsession, and Bear discovers the toy's price. Cooper Tomlinson, Megan Lawless, and Andy Richter appear in supporting roles.
