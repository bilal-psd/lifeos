M. Night Shyamalan writes and directs this crossover sequel to his earlier films Unbreakable and Split, closing out what became known as the Unbreakable trilogy. Bruce Willis and Samuel L. Jackson reprise their Unbreakable roles as David Dunn and Elijah Price, while James McAvoy returns as the multiple-personality character from Split. The three are confined together in a psychiatric facility, where the truth of their apparent superhuman abilities comes into question.
