No Country for Old Men, written and directed by Joel and Ethan Coen and released in 2007, is adapted from Cormac McCarthy's 2005 novel. In 1980 West Texas, welder and Vietnam veteran Llewelyn Moss finds two million dollars and a stash of heroin at the site of a drug deal gone wrong, drawing the attention of hitman Anton Chigurh while sheriff Ed Tom Bell tries to get ahead of them both. The film won four Academy Awards, including Best Picture.
