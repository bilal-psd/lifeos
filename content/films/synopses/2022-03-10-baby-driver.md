Ansel Elgort plays Baby, a getaway driver coerced into working for a crime boss, in this 2017 film written and directed by Edgar Wright, who first conceived the project in the mid-1990s. Baby wants out of that life for the sake of his girlfriend Debora, played by Lily James, but is pulled into one more heist alongside crew members played by Kevin Spacey, Jon Hamm, and Jamie Foxx. Wright set the film in Atlanta after the production secured Georgia tax credits.
