Onward (2020) follows two teenage elf brothers, Ian and Barley Lightfoot, in a suburban world where magic has mostly faded out of daily life. Directed by Dan Scanlon for Pixar, the story sends the brothers searching for an artifact that can bring their dead father back for a single day. Scanlon based the premise on his own father, who died in a car accident when Scanlon and his older brother were young children.
