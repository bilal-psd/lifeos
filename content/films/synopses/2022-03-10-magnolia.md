Magnolia (1999) follows a group of loosely connected characters across a single day in the San Fernando Valley, among them a dying man, his younger wife, a police officer, a former quiz-show prodigy and the host of the show that made him famous. Paul Thomas Anderson wrote and directed the film, drawing on the music of Aimee Mann while writing it. The cast includes Tom Cruise, Julianne Moore, William H. Macy, Philip Seymour Hoffman and John C. Reilly.
