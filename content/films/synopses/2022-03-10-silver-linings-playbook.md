Pat Solitano, played by Bradley Cooper, is a man with bipolar disorder released from a psychiatric hospital into his parents' home in Lansdowne, Pennsylvania, in this 2012 film written and directed by David O. Russell, adapted from Matthew Quick's 2008 novel The Silver Linings Playbook. Determined to win back his estranged wife, Pat meets Tiffany Maxwell, a young widow played by Jennifer Lawrence, who offers to help if he trains with her for a dance competition. Robert De Niro and Jacki Weaver play his parents.
