Matthew Vaughn directs this 2014 spy action comedy, based on the comic series The Secret Service and the first film in the Kingsman franchise. Colin Firth's veteran agent Harry Hart recruits Taron Egerton's Eggsy Unwin, an unpolished street kid, into the secret Kingsman organization and its competitive training program. Together with Mark Strong's Merlin, they work to stop Samuel L. Jackson's tech billionaire Richmond Valentine, whose scheme threatens the world.
