Tessa, an unconventional young woman played by Parvathy, becomes drawn into a chase through the back lanes of Kerala while searching for a mysterious artist, Charlie, who once lived in her apartment, in this 2015 Malayalam-language film directed by Martin Prakkat. Dulquer Salmaan co-stars; Prakkat wrote the screenplay with Unni R. Released on 24 December, the film won eight awards at the 46th Kerala State Film Awards, including Best Actor, Best Actress, and Best Director, and was later remade in Bengali, Marathi, and Tamil.
