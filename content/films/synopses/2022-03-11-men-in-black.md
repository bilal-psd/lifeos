Barry Sonnenfeld directs this 1997 science-fiction comedy, based on the Marvel Comics series by Lowell Cunningham and written by Ed Solomon. After a chase with an alien being, a New York City police officer, played by Will Smith, is recruited into the Men in Black, a secret agency that monitors extraterrestrial activity on Earth. Paired with veteran Agent K, played by Tommy Lee Jones, he investigates a plot to assassinate two ambassadors from rival galaxies.
