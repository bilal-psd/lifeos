Josh and Benny Safdie direct this crime thriller, written with Ronald Bronstein. Adam Sandler plays Howard Ratner, a jeweler and gambling addict in New York City who must retrieve an opal to settle his debts while juggling his business, his family, and a string of adversaries closing in around him. Kevin Garnett, LaKeith Stanfield, Julia Fox, and Idina Menzel round out the cast. Daniel Lopatin composed the score, and A24 released the film in December 2019 before Netflix distributed it internationally.
