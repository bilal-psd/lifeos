Tim Miller directs this 2016 film in his feature debut, the eighth entry in the X-Men series. Former Special Forces operative Wade Wilson survives a rogue experiment that leaves him scarred but able to heal rapidly, and adopts the identity of Deadpool to hunt down the man responsible. Ryan Reynolds stars alongside Morena Baccarin and T.J. Miller. Principal photography took place in Vancouver over about two months in 2015.
