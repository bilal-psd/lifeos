Peyton Reed directs this 2018 sequel to Ant-Man, the twentieth film in the Marvel Cinematic Universe. With his house arrest nearing its end, Scott Lang joins Hope van Dyne and Hank Pym on a mission into the Quantum Realm to rescue Janet van Dyne, who has been trapped there for decades. Paul Rudd, Evangeline Lilly, Michael Douglas, and Michelle Pfeiffer star. The film treats Lang and van Dyne as equal partners rather than sidekick and mentor.
