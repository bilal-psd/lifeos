Louis Leterrier directs this 2008 film, the second in the Marvel Cinematic Universe, from a script by Zak Penn. Scientist Bruce Banner searches for a cure to the rage that transforms him into the Hulk while a military program that helped create him tries to capture and weaponize his condition. Edward Norton stars as Banner alongside Liv Tyler, Tim Roth, and William Hurt. Mark Ruffalo took over the role for later Marvel films beginning with The Avengers in 2012.
