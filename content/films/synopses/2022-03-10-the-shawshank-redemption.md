The Shawshank Redemption, written and directed by Frank Darabont, adapts Stephen King's novella Rita Hayworth and Shawshank Redemption. Tim Robbins plays banker Andy Dufresne, sentenced to life at Shawshank State Penitentiary for the murders of his wife and her lover despite maintaining his innocence, who over two decades befriends fellow inmate Ellis Red Redding, played by Morgan Freeman. It earned only $16 million on release before becoming one of the most rented videos of 1995.
