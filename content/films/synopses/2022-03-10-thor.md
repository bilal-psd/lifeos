Kenneth Branagh directs this 2011 film, the fourth in the Marvel Cinematic Universe. After reigniting a dormant war against his father Odin's wishes, the arrogant warrior Thor is banished to Earth and stripped of his hammer, Mjolnir, forcing him to learn what it means to be a hero while his brother Loki schemes for the Asgardian throne. Chris Hemsworth stars in the title role alongside Natalie Portman, Tom Hiddleston, and Anthony Hopkins. The film was converted to 3D in post-production.
