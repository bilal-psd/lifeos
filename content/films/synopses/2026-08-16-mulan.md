When Imperial China orders one man from every family into the army to fight off invading Huns, Mulan disguises herself as a soldier to take her ailing father's place, in this 1998 Walt Disney animated musical directed by Barry Cook and Tony Bancroft and based on the Chinese legend of Hua Mulan. Ming-Na Wen and Eddie Murphy voice Mulan and Mushu in the English version, while Jackie Chan voiced Li Shang for the Chinese dub.
