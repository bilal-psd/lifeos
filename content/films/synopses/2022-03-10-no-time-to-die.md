Bond has left active service for a quiet life in Jamaica when his old CIA contact Felix Leiter asks for help extracting a kidnapped scientist, in this 2021 film, the twenty-fifth in the James Bond series and Daniel Craig's fifth and final appearance as Bond. The mission draws Bond toward bioterrorist Lyutsifer Safin, who is developing DNA-targeting nanobot weapons. Léa Seydoux and Ben Whishaw return from earlier films. Cary Joji Fukunaga directs and co-wrote the screenplay with Neal Purvis, Robert Wade, and Phoebe Waller-Bridge.
