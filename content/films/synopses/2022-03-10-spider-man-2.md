Sam Raimi's 2004 sequel catches Peter Parker exhausted by his double life, ready to set aside being Spider-Man even as Doc Ock's rampage leaves the city suffering. Parker struggles to balance his job and studies with his unspoken feelings for Mary Jane Watson, while his friend Harry Osborn's resentment toward him builds. Alvin Sargent wrote the screenplay from a story credited to Michael Chabon, Alfred Gough, and Miles Millar.
