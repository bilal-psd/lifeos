Kishkindha Kaandam (2024) is a Malayalam mystery thriller directed by Dinjith Ayyathan and written by Bahul Ramesh, set in the monkey-inhabited Kallepathi reserve forest. Asif Ali plays Ajay Chandran, a forest officer who lives with his ex-military father, Appu Pillai, played by Vijayaraghavan, as they and a newlywed couple investigate a string of strange disturbances in the area. Aparna Balamurali also stars. The film is the first of Bahul Ramesh's Animal Trilogy, followed by Kerala Crime Files 2 and Eko in 2025.
