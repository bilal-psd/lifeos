Cate Shortland directs this 2021 film, the twenty-fourth in the Marvel Cinematic Universe, from a screenplay by Eric Pearson. Set largely during the events of Captain America: Civil War, it follows Natasha Romanoff as a conspiracy tied to her past as a Russian spy forces her to confront relationships she left behind before becoming an Avenger. Scarlett Johansson stars alongside Florence Pugh, David Harbour, and Rachel Weisz. Its release was delayed three times by the COVID-19 pandemic.
