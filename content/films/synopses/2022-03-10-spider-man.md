Sam Raimi directs this 2002 origin film, the first in his Spider-Man trilogy, from a screenplay by David Koepp. A spider genetically altered at Oscorp bites the teenager Peter Parker, played by Tobey Maguire, giving him extraordinary abilities. Parker adopts the identity of Spider-Man and fights crime in New York City, where he confronts the Green Goblin, played by Willem Dafoe. Kirsten Dunst and James Franco round out the cast.
