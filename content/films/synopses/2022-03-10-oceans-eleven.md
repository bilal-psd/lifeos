Steven Soderbergh directs this heist comedy, a remake of the 1960 Rat Pack film and the first installment in the Ocean's trilogy. George Clooney plays Danny Ocean, who teams with Rusty Ryan, played by Brad Pitt, to plan the theft of $160 million from three Las Vegas casinos owned by Terry Benedict, played by Andy Garcia, who happens to be involved with Ocean's ex-wife, Tess, played by Julia Roberts. Matt Damon, Don Cheadle, and Casey Affleck round out the crew.
