Manu Ashokan directs this Malayalam drama, his directorial debut, written by Bobby and Sanjay. Parvathy Thiruvothu plays Pallavi Raveendran, an aviation student whose ambition to become a pilot is threatened after her boyfriend attacks her with acid. Asif Ali and Tovino Thomas co-star, and the film was produced by three sisters, Shenuga, Shegna, and Sherga. Ashokan's debut was nominated for Best Debut Feature Film of a director at the International Film Festival of India.
