Chris Williams and Don Hall direct this animated film from Walt Disney Animation Studios, loosely based on the Marvel Comics superhero team of the same name. Ryan Potter voices Hiro Hamada, a robotics prodigy who bonds with Baymax, a healthcare robot built by his older brother Tadashi. After Tadashi dies, Hiro and his friends assemble a team of makeshift superheroes to track down the figure responsible. It is the first Disney animated feature to include Marvel characters.
