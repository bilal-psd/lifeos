Vito Corleone, played by Marlon Brando, is the patriarch of a Mafia family in this 1972 film directed by Francis Ford Coppola and adapted with Mario Puzo from Puzo's best-selling 1969 novel. His youngest son Michael, played by Al Pacino, is drawn from reluctant outsider into the family business after an attempt on his father's life. James Caan, Robert Duvall, and Diane Keaton also star. Paramount Pictures secured the film rights for 80,000 dollars before the novel became popular.
