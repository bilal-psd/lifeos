Anna Boden and Ryan Fleck direct this 2019 film, the twenty-first in the Marvel Cinematic Universe and Marvel Studios' first female-led project. Set in 1995, it follows Carol Danvers as she becomes Captain Marvel after Earth is drawn into a war between the Kree and the Skrulls. Brie Larson stars alongside Samuel L. Jackson and Jude Law. Two actors, Jackson and Clark Gregg, were digitally de-aged to match the film's 1990s setting.
