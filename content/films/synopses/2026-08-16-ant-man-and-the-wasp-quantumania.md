Peyton Reed directs this 2023 film, the thirty-first in the Marvel Cinematic Universe, from a script by Jeff Loveness. Scott Lang, Hope van Dyne, her parents Janet and Hank, and Scott's daughter Cassie are pulled into the Quantum Realm, where they encounter new creatures and confront Kang the Conqueror. Paul Rudd and Evangeline Lilly return, joined by Jonathan Majors and Bill Murray. The production budget of $330.1 million made it one of the costliest Marvel films made.
