David Fincher directs this 2010 biographical drama, written by Aaron Sorkin and based on Ben Mezrich's 2009 book The Accidental Billionaires. Jesse Eisenberg plays Mark Zuckerberg, who begins building what becomes Facebook as a Harvard undergraduate in 2003 and, within six years, is one of the youngest billionaires in history, his success shadowed by two lawsuits, including one from his former friend and co-founder Eduardo Saverin, played by Andrew Garfield. Justin Timberlake plays Sean Parker.
