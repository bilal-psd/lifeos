Before Sunrise, directed by Richard Linklater and released in 1995, is the first film in the Before trilogy. Jesse, played by Ethan Hawke, and Céline, played by Julie Delpy, meet on a train and get off together in Vienna, where they spend the night walking the city and talking about life, love, time, and death, aware that the night may be all they get.
