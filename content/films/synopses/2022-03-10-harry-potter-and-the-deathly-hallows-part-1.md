Harry, Ron Weasley, and Hermione Granger leave Hogwarts to search for and destroy Voldemort's Horcruxes in this 2010 film, the first of two parts adapting J.K. Rowling's final novel. David Yates directs from a Steve Kloves screenplay, continuing the story from Harry Potter and the Half-Blood Prince as Death Eaters grow bolder across the wizarding world. It is the seventh instalment in the Harry Potter film series, released in both standard and IMAX formats.
