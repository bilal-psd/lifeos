Aashiq Abu directs and co-produces this Malayalam romantic thriller, written by Syam Pushkaran and Dileesh Nair. Tovino Thomas plays Mathan, a man on the run after a money deal goes wrong and he kills a police officer. He reconnects in Kochi with Aparna, played by Aishwarya Lekshmi, an old flame now struggling to establish herself as an actress, as their rekindled relationship unfolds alongside an intensifying police hunt. The film's plot draws inspiration from the 1960 French film Breathless.
