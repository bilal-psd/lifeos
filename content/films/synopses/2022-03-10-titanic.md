James Cameron writes and directs this 1997 romance, which frames the sinking of the RMS Titanic through the memory of 101-year-old survivor Rose DeWitt Bukater, played by Kate Winslet in her older years and in her youth aboard the ship. The young Rose boards with her mother and fiance, while Jack Dawson, played by Leonardo DiCaprio, wins a third-class ticket for the same voyage. Their story runs through the ship's only voyage, which ends in disaster on April 15, 1912.
