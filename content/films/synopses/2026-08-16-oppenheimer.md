Christopher Nolan wrote and directed this 2023 drama, adapting Kai Bird and Martin J. Sherwin's biography American Prometheus. Cillian Murphy plays J. Robert Oppenheimer, who ran the Los Alamos laboratory that built the first atomic bomb. The film cuts between that work and the 1954 security hearing that followed, shooting one thread in colour and the other in black and white. It won seven Academy Awards, including Best Picture.
