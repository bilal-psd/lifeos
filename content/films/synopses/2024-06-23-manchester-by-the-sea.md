Manchester by the Sea (2016) follows Lee Chandler, played by Casey Affleck, who returns to his hometown after his brother dies and becomes the legal guardian of his sixteen-year-old nephew. The film, written and directed by Kenneth Lonergan, forces Lee to confront a past tragedy that had driven him from his family and community. Michelle Williams plays his former wife and Kyle Chandler his late brother. It premiered at the Sundance Film Festival in January 2016, filmed the year before in Manchester, Massachusetts.
