Anand Ekarshi writes and directs this Malayalam thriller. After a party, Anjali, the sole actress in a theatre group, is subjected to a crime by one of the men in the group, prompting a meeting where the members try to reach a consensus. As the discussion continues, suspicions surface and old tensions come out. The film won the Grand Jury Award at the Indian Film Festival of Los Angeles and the National Film Award for Best Feature Film.
