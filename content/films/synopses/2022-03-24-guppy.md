Guppy (2016), directed by Johnpaul George, is a Malayalam-language drama set in a seaside village. Chethan Jayalal plays Guppy, a teenager who raises and sells guppy fish to save money for a motorized wheelchair for his ailing mother, played by Rohini. Tension builds after an engineer named Thejus Varkey, played by Tovino Thomas, arrives to oversee construction of a railway bridge through the village, and a rivalry develops between the two. The film was released on 5 August 2016.
