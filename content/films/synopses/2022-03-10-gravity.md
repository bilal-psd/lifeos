Gravity, directed by Alfonso Cuarón and released in 2013, follows Dr. Ryan Stone, an engineer on her first space mission, played by Sandra Bullock, and Matt Kowalski, an astronaut on his last expedition, played by George Clooney, after debris destroys their Space Shuttle during a spacewalk. The visual effects, covering more than eighty of the film's ninety one minutes, took British company Framestore over three years to complete. Cuarón won the Academy Award for Best Director.
