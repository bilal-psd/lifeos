Matthew Vaughn returns to direct this 2017 sequel to Kingsman: The Secret Service. After an attack destroys the Kingsman headquarters, Taron Egerton's Eggsy and Mark Strong's Merlin join forces with the American spy agency Statesman, whose ranks include Channing Tatum and Jeff Bridges, to track down Julianne Moore's drug lord Poppy Adams and her cartel, the Golden Circle. Colin Firth returns as Harry Hart.
