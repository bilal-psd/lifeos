Steven Spielberg directs this dramatization of the true story of Oskar Schindler, a German industrialist who saved more than a thousand Polish Jewish refugees from the Holocaust by employing them in his factories, based on Thomas Keneally's 1982 novel Schindler's Ark. Liam Neeson plays Schindler, with Ralph Fiennes as SS officer Amon Goeth. Shot in black and white, the film won seven Academy Awards, including Best Picture.
