Andy Muschietti directs this first half of a two-part adaptation of Stephen King's 1986 novel. Set in the fictional town of Derry, Maine, it follows seven outcast children known as the Losers' Club as they are terrorized by a shapeshifting entity that emerges from the sewers as Pennywise the Dancing Clown, played by Bill Skarsgard. The film grossed over $704 million worldwide, becoming the highest-grossing horror film ever at the time.
