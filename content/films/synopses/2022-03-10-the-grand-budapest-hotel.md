The Grand Budapest Hotel, written and directed by Wes Anderson, follows Monsieur Gustave H., played by Ralph Fiennes, a concierge at a mountain resort in the fictional country of Zubrowka, and his apprentice Zero, played by Tony Revolori. After Gustave is framed for the murder of a wealthy guest, the two set out to recover a priceless Renaissance painting and claim a fortune amid a rising fascist regime.
