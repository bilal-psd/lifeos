Peter Weir directs this 1998 comedy-drama, from a script by Andrew Niccol originally written as a science-fiction thriller set in New York before being reworked for the film. Jim Carrey plays Truman Burbank, an insurance salesman in a picture-perfect seaside town who begins to suspect that his entire life has been broadcast to an audience without his knowledge. Ed Harris and Laura Linney appear in supporting roles. Brian De Palma was originally set to direct.
