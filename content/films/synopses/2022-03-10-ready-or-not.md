Matt Bettinelli-Olpin and Tyler Gillett direct this 2019 comedy horror film, written by Guy Busick and R. Christopher Murphy. Samara Weaving plays a bride who marries into a wealthy family, only to learn on her wedding night that tradition requires new members to play a game before dawn. Her in-laws, including characters played by Andie MacDowell and Henry Czerny, turn the game into a hunt for her life.
