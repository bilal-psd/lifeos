Dune, directed by Denis Villeneuve and released in 2021, adapts the first half of Frank Herbert's 1965 novel. Timothée Chalamet plays Paul Atreides, whose noble family, House Atreides, takes control of the desert planet Arrakis and its supply of a resource that grants extraordinary power to those who control it. The move draws the family into a war with rival forces who want the planet for themselves.
