Lijo Jose Pellissery directs this 2017 Malayalam-language crime drama, written by Chemban Vinod Jose and cast almost entirely with debut actors. Antony Varghese plays Vincent Pepe, who leads friends starting a pork business in the town of Angamaly. The group strikes a deal with two men, Rajan and Ravi, despite knowing they killed a friend of theirs in a gang fight, complicating Vincent's plan to leave the country with his girlfriend Sakhi. The climax runs as a single unbroken eleven-minute take.
