Lost Ladies, known in India as Laapataa Ladies, is a 2023 Hindi film directed by Kiran Rao. Set in 2001 in rural India, it follows two young brides accidentally swapped on a train, each ending up with a family that is not her own, and the string of characters they meet while trying to sort out the mix up. It was India's official entry for Best International Feature Film at the 97th Academy Awards, though it was not nominated.
