Aditya Chopra writes and directs this Hindi romantic musical, his directorial debut, produced by his father Yash Chopra. Shah Rukh Khan plays Raj, a carefree, wealthy non-resident Indian, and Kajol plays Simran, whose father has already promised her hand to a family friend's son. After the two fall in love during a European vacation, Raj follows Simran to India, determined to win over her family before her arranged wedding, in a story shot across India, London, and Switzerland.
