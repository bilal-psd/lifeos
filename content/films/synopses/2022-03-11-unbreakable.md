M. Night Shyamalan writes, produces, and directs this film starting the trilogy he would later complete with Split and Glass. Bruce Willis plays David Dunn, a security guard who survives a train crash without a scratch and is drawn into the orbit of Elijah Price, played by Samuel L. Jackson, a comic-book store owner with a disease that leaves his bones fragile as glass. The film developed a lasting cult following.
