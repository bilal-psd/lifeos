David Leitch directs this 2018 sequel, the eleventh installment in the X-Men film series. Wade Wilson forms the mutant team X-Force to protect a young mutant, Russell, from Cable, a soldier who has traveled back in time. Ryan Reynolds reprises the title role and also co-wrote the script, alongside Josh Brolin as Cable and Zazie Beetz. It became the highest-grossing film in the X-Men series at the time of its release.
