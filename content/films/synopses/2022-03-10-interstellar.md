Christopher Nolan directs this 2014 science fiction film, co-written with his brother Jonathan Nolan. Matthew McConaughey and Anne Hathaway lead a crew of astronauts who travel through a newly discovered wormhole in search of a habitable world, as Earth suffers a worsening blight and famine. Physicist Kip Thorne, who helped originate the project in 2006, served as scientific consultant and later wrote a companion book on the film's science.
