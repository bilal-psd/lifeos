The Theory of Everything, directed by James Marsh and released in 2014, is adapted from Jane Hawking's memoir Travelling to Infinity: My Life with Stephen, about her marriage to physicist Stephen Hawking. Eddie Redmayne plays Hawking, who receives a diagnosis of motor neurone disease at twenty one while a student at Cambridge, and Felicity Jones plays Jane, who stays at his side as he pursues his most ambitious scientific work. Redmayne won the Academy Award for Best Actor for the role.
