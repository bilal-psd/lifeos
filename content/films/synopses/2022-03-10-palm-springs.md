Max Barbakow directs this 2020 science-fiction romantic comedy, his feature debut, written by Andy Siara from a story the two developed together. Andy Samberg, who also produced, plays Nyles, a guest at a Palm Springs wedding who meets Sarah, the reluctant maid of honor played by Cristin Milioti, and the pair find themselves unable to leave the venue, caught in a time loop they cannot escape. J. K. Simmons also stars. The film premiered at the 2020 Sundance Film Festival.
