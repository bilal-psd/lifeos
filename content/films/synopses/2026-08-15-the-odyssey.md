The Odyssey (2026) is Christopher Nolan's adaptation of Homer's epic poem, following Odysseus, played by Matt Damon, as he tries to make his way home to Ithaca after the Trojan War. Along the way he encounters mythical beings while attempting to reunite with his wife Penelope, played by Anne Hathaway. Nolan wrote and directed the film, producing it with his wife Emma Thomas, and studied several translations of the poem before finishing the script. The cast also includes Tom Holland and Zendaya.
