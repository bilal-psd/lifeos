Louis Leterrier directs this 2013 heist film, the first in the Now You See Me series. Jesse Eisenberg, Woody Harrelson, Isla Fisher and Dave Franco play a group of magicians who rob banks during their live shows and give the stolen money to their audiences. Mark Ruffalo's FBI agent and Melanie Laurent's Interpol detective join forces to track them down. Michael Caine and Morgan Freeman round out the cast.
