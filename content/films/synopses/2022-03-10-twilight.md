Catherine Hardwicke directs this first film in the Twilight Saga, adapting Stephenie Meyer's 2005 novel. Kristen Stewart plays Bella Swan, who moves to a small town in the Pacific Northwest and falls for her classmate Edward Cullen, played by Robert Pattinson, after learning he is a 108-year-old vampire. Released in November 2008 to mixed reviews from critics, the film grossed $412 million worldwide.
