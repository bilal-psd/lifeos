Crazy, Stupid, Love. (2011) follows Cal Weaver, played by Steve Carell, whose wife Emily asks for a divorce after admitting she's been unfaithful. In his forties and newly single, Cal is taken under the wing of a younger, more practiced bachelor named Jacob Palmer, played by Ryan Gosling, who tries to teach him how to date again. Glenn Ficarra and John Requa directed the film from a script by Dan Fogelman, with Julianne Moore, Emma Stone and Marisa Tomei in supporting roles.
