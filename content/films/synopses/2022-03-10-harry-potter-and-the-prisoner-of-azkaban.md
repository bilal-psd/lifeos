Sirius Black, a wizard believed loyal to Voldemort, escapes from Azkaban Prison and heads toward Hogwarts in this 2004 film, putting Harry Potter in danger once again. Alfonso Cuarón directs, taking over from Chris Columbus for this sequel to Harry Potter and the Chamber of Secrets, as Harry searches for the truth about his past and about Black's connection to his parents. With this film the series moved to a longer eighteen-month production schedule.
