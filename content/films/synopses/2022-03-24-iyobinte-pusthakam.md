Amal Neerad directs this 2014 Malayalam-language film, whose title translates to Book of Job, written by Gopan Chithambaran with dialogue by Syam Pushkaran. Set in the mid-20th century in Munnar, in the Western Ghats, it follows Iyob, a former slave who becomes a ruthless businessman, and the rivalry among his sons Aloshy, Ivan, and Dimitri, names drawn from Fyodor Dostoyevsky's The Brothers Karamazov. Fahadh Faasil, Lal, Jayasurya, and Isha Sharvani lead the cast, with a cameo from Aashiq Abu.
