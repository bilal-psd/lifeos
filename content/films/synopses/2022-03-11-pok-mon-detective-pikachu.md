Pokémon Detective Pikachu (2019) is the first live-action film based on the Pokémon franchise, loosely adapted from the 2016 video game of the same name. Rob Letterman directed the film, which follows a talking Pikachu, voiced by Ryan Reynolds, who teams up with a young man named Tim Goodman, played by Justice Smith, to investigate the disappearance of Tim's father, Harry. Kathryn Newton and Suki Waterhouse also star as the pair search for answers in a world shared by people and Pokémon.
