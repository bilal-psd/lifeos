Don Hall and Carlos López Estrada direct this 2021 animated film from Walt Disney Animation Studios, written by Qui Nguyen and Adele Lim. In the fantasy land of Kumandra, where humans and dragons once lived together before dragons sacrificed themselves to defeat an evil force, warrior Raya, voiced by Kelly Marie Tran, sets out five hundred years later to find the last dragon, Sisu, voiced by Awkwafina, after that evil returns and threatens her father and her fractured homeland.
