Scott Derrickson directs this 2016 film, the fourteenth in the Marvel Cinematic Universe, from a script he wrote with Jon Spaihts and C. Robert Cargill. After a car crash ends his career as a neurosurgeon, Stephen Strange travels to Nepal, where a sorcerer trains him in the mystic arts to defend the world against a hidden threat. Benedict Cumberbatch stars alongside Tilda Swinton, Chiwetel Ejiofor, and Rachel McAdams.
