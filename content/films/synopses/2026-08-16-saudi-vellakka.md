Tharun Moorthy writes and directs this Malayalam crime drama, formally titled Saudi Vellakka CC.225/2009, starring Lukman Avaran as Abhilash Sasidharan, who receives a court summons connected to an incident from his childhood. Investigating the case, he uncovers how a small dispute grew into a conflict that changed the course of everyone involved. Produced by Sandip Senan under Urvasi Theatres, the film was released on December 2, 2022, to positive reviews, and went on to win the National Film Award for Best Malayalam Feature Film.
