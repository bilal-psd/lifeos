Teenagers Irshad and Haseeb share a passion for competitive pigeon flying in this 2017 Malayalam-language film set in Mattanchery, the directorial debut of Soubin Shahir, who wrote the script with Muneer Ali. As the story unfolds, Irshad reveals a tragic incident from the past that broke apart several friendships in the neighborhood. Dulquer Salmaan, Shane Nigam, and Amal Shah star. Principal photography began at Fort Kochi on 1 June 2016, and the film was released on 21 September 2017.
