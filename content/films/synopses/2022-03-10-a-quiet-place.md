John Krasinski directs and co-writes this 2018 post-apocalyptic horror film, working from a story by Scott Beck and Bryan Woods. Krasinski and Emily Blunt play parents raising two children, portrayed by Millicent Simmonds and Noah Jupe, in a world overrun by blind extraterrestrial creatures with an acute sense of hearing. The family survives largely by staying silent, since any sound can draw the creatures toward them.
