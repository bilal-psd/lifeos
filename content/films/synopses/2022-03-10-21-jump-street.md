Phil Lord and Christopher Miller direct this adaptation of the 1987 to 1991 television series. Jonah Hill and Channing Tatum play police officers Schmidt and Jenko, sent undercover as students at a high school to shut down a drug ring supplying a new synthetic substance. Written by Michael Bacall from a story he developed with Hill, the film premiered at South by Southwest.
