Todd Phillips directs this 2009 comedy, the first film in The Hangover trilogy, written by Jon Lucas and Scott Moore. Phil, Stu, and Alan, played by Bradley Cooper, Ed Helms, and Zach Galifianakis, travel to Las Vegas for the bachelor party of their friend Doug, played by Justin Bartha, and wake up the next morning with no memory of the night before and no sign of Doug. The trio retraces their steps to find him before the wedding.
