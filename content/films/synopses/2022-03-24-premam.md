Alphonse Puthren writes, directs, and edits this 2015 Malayalam film. The story follows George across three stages of his life and the three women he encounters along the way, from a disappointing first love to Malar, a college lecturer who rekindles his interest. Nivin Pauly stars as George opposite Sai Pallavi, in her major film debut as Malar. The film was shot across Goa, Aluva, and Fort Kochi.
