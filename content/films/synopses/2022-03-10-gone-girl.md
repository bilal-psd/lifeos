Gone Girl, directed by David Fincher and released in 2014, is adapted from Gillian Flynn's 2012 novel of the same name. Ben Affleck plays Nick Dunne, a writing teacher in Missouri who becomes the prime suspect after his wife, Amy, played by Rosamund Pike, disappears and the case turns into a media event. Pike received an Academy Award nomination for the role, and the film became Fincher's highest grossing to date.
