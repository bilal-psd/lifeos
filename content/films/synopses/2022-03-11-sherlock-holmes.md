Guy Ritchie directs this period mystery, starring Robert Downey Jr. as Sherlock Holmes and Jude Law as Dr. Watson, set in London in 1890. The pair investigate Lord Blackwood, played by Mark Strong, a practitioner of the occult who appears to have returned from the dead and threatens a wider plot against England. Rachel McAdams appears as Irene Adler, Holmes's former adversary. The screenplay was written by Michael Robert Johnson, Anthony Peckham, and Simon Kinberg, from a story by Johnson and producer Lionel Wigram.
