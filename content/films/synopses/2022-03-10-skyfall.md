MI6 headquarters comes under attack and agents around the world are exposed after one of Bond's assignments goes badly wrong, in this 2012 film directed by Sam Mendes. Bond, aided by field agent Eve, tracks down the person behind it, Raoul Silva, played by Javier Bardem, while M, played by Judi Dench, faces pressure over her position from Gareth Mallory, the new chairman overseeing MI6. Miss Moneypenny and Q, played by Naomie Harris and Ben Whishaw, reappear after a two-film absence.
