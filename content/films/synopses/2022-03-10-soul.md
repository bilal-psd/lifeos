Soul (2020) follows Joe Gardner, a middle school band teacher and aspiring jazz pianist, whose soul is separated from his body in an accident just after he lands his big break at the Half Note Club. He ends up in the You Seminar, where souls develop their personalities before being sent to newborns, and enlists a soul named 22 to help him get back to his body. Pete Docter directed the film for Pixar with Mike Jones and Kemp Powers.
