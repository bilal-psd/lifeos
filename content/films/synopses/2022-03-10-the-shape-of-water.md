Guillermo del Toro directs and produces this 2017 film, co-written with Vanessa Taylor and inspired by Creature from the Black Lagoon. Set in Baltimore in 1962 during the Cold War, it follows a mute cleaner at a high-security government laboratory who falls in love with a captive amphibious creature and sets out to help him escape before a government colonel can kill him. Sally Hawkins and Michael Shannon star. It premiered at the Venice International Film Festival, where it won the Golden Lion.
