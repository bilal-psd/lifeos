Lana and Lilly Wachowski write and direct this 1999 science-fiction action film, the first in the Matrix series. Keanu Reeves plays Neo, a computer hacker recruited by Morpheus, played by Laurence Fishburne, into a rebellion against the machines that secretly run a simulated reality called the Matrix, in which most of humanity is unknowingly trapped. Carrie-Anne Moss, Hugo Weaving, and Joe Pantoliano also star. The fight choreography draws on anime, martial arts cinema, and Hong Kong wire fu.
