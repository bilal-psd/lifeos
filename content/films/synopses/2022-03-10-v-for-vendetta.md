V for Vendetta, directed by James McTeigue from a script by the Wachowskis, adapts Alan Moore and David Lloyd's graphic novel. Set in a future Britain ruled by a fascist regime, it follows a masked vigilante known as V, played by Hugo Weaving, who rescues a young woman from the secret police and finds in her an ally for his campaign against the government.
