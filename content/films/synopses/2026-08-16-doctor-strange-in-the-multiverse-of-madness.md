Sam Raimi directs this 2022 film, the twenty-eighth in the Marvel Cinematic Universe and sequel to Doctor Strange. Stephen Strange enlists old and new allies to protect America Chavez, a teenager able to travel between universes, from the Scarlet Witch. Benedict Cumberbatch stars alongside Elizabeth Olsen and Xochitl Gomez. Raimi brought horror elements to the story, continuing Wanda Maximoff's arc from the Disney+ series WandaVision.
