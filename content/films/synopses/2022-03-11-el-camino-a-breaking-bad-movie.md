El Camino: A Breaking Bad Movie (2019) picks up right after Jesse Pinkman's escape from captivity at the end of the Breaking Bad television series, following him as he tries to reckon with his past and find a way forward. Vince Gilligan, the creator of Breaking Bad, wrote and directed the film in his feature directorial debut, and Aaron Paul reprises his role as Jesse. Several other cast members return as well, including Jesse Plemons, Krysten Ritter and Bryan Cranston.
