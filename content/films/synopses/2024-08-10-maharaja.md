Nithilan Saminathan directs this 2024 Tamil-language thriller, marking actor Vijay Sethupathi's fiftieth film as a lead. Sethupathi plays a barber in Chennai who reports a burglary at his home and tells police that his "Lakshmi" was taken, leaving them unsure whether he means a person or an object. Anurag Kashyap co-stars, and Sachana Namidass appears in her first major film role. The police dismiss him at first, before learning what is actually missing. B. Ajaneesh Loknath composed the music.
