Johny Antony directs this 2003 Malayalam action comedy, written by Udaykrishna and Sibi K. Thomas. Moolamkuzhiyil Sahadevan, known as CID Moosa, works as a private detective from a family of police officers and takes on a string of cases, with his sharpest rivalry reserved for his brother-in-law, police officer Peethambaran. Dileep stars in the title role alongside Bhavana and Jagathy Sreekumar. The film was remade in Tamil, Kannada, and Telugu.
