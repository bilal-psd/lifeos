The second installment of Peter Jackson's Lord of the Rings trilogy, based on the middle volume of Tolkien's novel, follows three separated storylines. Frodo and Sam press toward Mordor guided by Gollum, while Merry and Pippin escape their orc captors and meet the Ent Treebeard near Isengard. Aragorn, Legolas, and Gimli travel to Rohan and reunite with Gandalf before joining King Theoden's army at the Battle of Helm's Deep. It grossed $923 million worldwide and won two Academy Awards.
