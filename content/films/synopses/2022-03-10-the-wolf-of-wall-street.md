Martin Scorsese directs this 2013 film, adapted by Terence Winter from Jordan Belfort's 2007 memoir. Leonardo DiCaprio plays Belfort, a stockbroker whose firm, Stratton Oakmont, built its fortune on securities fraud. Jonah Hill plays his business partner Donnie Azoff, Margot Robbie plays his wife Naomi, and Matthew McConaughey plays Mark Hanna, the boss who mentors Belfort early in his career. Kyle Chandler plays the FBI agent investigating him as the fraud unravels.
