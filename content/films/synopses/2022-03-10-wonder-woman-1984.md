Patty Jenkins returns to direct this sequel to 2017's Wonder Woman, set during the Cold War in 1984. Gal Gadot reprises Diana Prince alongside Chris Pine as her past love Steve Trevor, with Kristen Wiig and Pedro Pascal joining as Cheetah and Maxwell Lord. After a botched robbery pulls her into a wider conflict, Diana faces a mysterious ancient force that threatens her own powers as Lord's rise pushes the world toward disaster.
