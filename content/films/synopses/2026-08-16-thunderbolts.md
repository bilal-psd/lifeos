Jake Schreier directs this 2025 film, the thirty-sixth in the Marvel Cinematic Universe, from a script by Eric Pearson and Joanna Calo. Seven disillusioned antiheroes are trapped together and forced onto a mission that confronts each of them with their pasts. Florence Pugh, Sebastian Stan, and David Harbour star alongside Wyatt Russell and Julia Louis-Dreyfus. By the film's end the group rebrands itself, a twist reflected in the asterisk of its title.
