Will Hunting, played by Matt Damon, is a self-taught genius working as a janitor at MIT in Boston, whose mathematical talent is discovered by professor Gerald Lambeau, played by Stellan Skarsgård, in this 1997 drama directed by Gus Van Sant. To avoid jail, Will agrees to study under Lambeau while attending therapy with psychologist Sean Maguire, played by Robin Williams, forcing him to confront his past, including a relationship with Harvard student Skylar, played by Minnie Driver. Damon and Ben Affleck wrote the screenplay.
