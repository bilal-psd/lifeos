Shane Black directs this 2013 film, the seventh in the Marvel Cinematic Universe, from a script he co-wrote with Drew Pearce. After the events of The Avengers, Tony Stark rebuilds his life and pursues retribution against a terrorist known as the Mandarin, whose campaign targets the United States. Robert Downey Jr. stars alongside Gwyneth Paltrow, Don Cheadle, and Ben Kingsley. The script draws on Warren Ellis's comic book arc Extremis.
