Greta Gerwig's 2017 film follows Christine McPherson, who calls herself Lady Bird, through her final year of school in Sacramento. She is seventeen, strongly opinionated, and certain she belongs somewhere else. Most of the film sits in the friction between her and her mother, and in what she owes the place and the family she wants to leave.
