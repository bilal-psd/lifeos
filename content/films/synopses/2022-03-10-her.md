Spike Jonze writes and directs this science fiction romance starring Joaquin Phoenix as Theodore Twombly, a lonely writer who purchases an artificially intelligent operating system. He develops a relationship with the system, voiced by Scarlett Johansson as Samantha. Jonze wrote the screenplay in his solo screenwriting debut, earning the Academy Award for Best Original Screenplay among five nominations, including Best Picture.
