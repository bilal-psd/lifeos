Logan, directed by James Mangold, is the tenth film in the X-Men series and the third centered on Wolverine, played by Hugh Jackman. Set in the near future, it follows a weary Logan caring for an ailing Professor X at a hideout near the Mexican border, drawing loosely on the comic storyline Old Man Logan. Their isolation ends when a young mutant, Laura, arrives pursued by the Reavers, pulling Logan back into a fight he had tried to leave behind.
