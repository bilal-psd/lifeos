Gore Verbinski directs this third installment in the Pirates of the Caribbean series, a direct sequel to Dead Man's Chest. Will Turner, played by Orlando Bloom, Elizabeth Swann, played by Keira Knightley, and Hector Barbossa journey to Davy Jones' Locker to retrieve Jack Sparrow. As Lord Cutler Beckett and the East India Trading Company tighten their grip on the seas by controlling Davy Jones, the crew works to unite the pirate lords of the Brethren Court against him.
