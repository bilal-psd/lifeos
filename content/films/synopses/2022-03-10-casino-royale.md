Martin Campbell directs this 2006 film, the twenty-first Bond entry from Eon Productions and the first to cast Daniel Craig in the role. Newly promoted to 00 status, Bond is sent by M to stop Le Chiffre, played by Mads Mikkelsen, a financier for terrorist groups, from winning a high-stakes poker game at the Casino Royale in Montenegro. Eva Green plays Vesper Lynd, who assists him, alongside Jeffrey Wright's Felix Leiter. The film rebooted the series after Die Another Day.
