Denis Villeneuve directs this sequel to his 2021 film Dune, continuing the two-part adaptation of Frank Herbert's 1965 novel. Timothee Chalamet returns as Paul Atreides, who allies with Chani and the Fremen people to move against House Harkonnen after the destruction of his family, facing a growing pull toward becoming a messianic figure. The film grossed $715 million worldwide and received five Academy Award nominations, including Best Picture, winning two.
