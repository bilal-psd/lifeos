Baz Luhrmann co-writes and directs this adaptation of F. Scott Fitzgerald's 1925 novel, filmed in Australia on a $105 million budget. Tobey Maguire plays Nick Carraway, who narrates his experience of the parties and excess of 1922 Long Island as he becomes drawn into the orbit of his mysterious neighbor, Jay Gatsby, played by Leonardo DiCaprio, and Gatsby's pursuit of Daisy, played by Carey Mulligan. Joel Edgerton, Isla Fisher, and Elizabeth Debicki round out the cast.
