Basil Joseph directs this 2015 Malayalam-language comedy, his debut feature, scripted by Deepu Pradeep. Its title translates to The Story of Kunjiraman, and it follows Kunjiraman as he returns home from the Gulf hoping to settle into marriage, only for his cousin Laalu and a curse hanging over the village to get in the way. Vineeth Sreenivasan, Dhyan Sreenivasan, Aju Varghese, Neeraj Madhav, and Deepak Parambol lead the cast. The film was released on 28 August 2015.
