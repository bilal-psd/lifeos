Snowpiercer, directed by Bong Joon Ho and released in 2013, is adapted from the 1982 French graphic novel Le Transperceneige by Jacques Lob, Benjamin Legrand, and Jean Marc Rochette. After a failed attempt to reverse global warming kills off most life on Earth, the survivors live aboard a train called the Snowpiercer that circles the globe on a perpetual motion engine, with a rigid class system dividing the cars from front to back. The film later inspired a television series of the same name.
