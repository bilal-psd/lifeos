Ocean's Eight, directed by Gary Ross and released in 2018, is a spin off of Steven Soderbergh's Ocean's trilogy and the fourth film in the Ocean's franchise. Sandra Bullock plays Debbie Ocean, sister of Danny Ocean, who assembles a crew of women, including characters played by Cate Blanchett, Anne Hathaway, and Mindy Kaling, to steal a piece from the Met Gala at New York's Metropolitan Museum of Art. It opened exactly eleven years after Ocean's Thirteen.
