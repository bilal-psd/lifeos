The Imitation Game (2014), directed by Morten Tyldum, is based on the life of the cryptanalyst Alan Turing. Set during the Second World War, it follows Turing and his team of code-breakers at Britain's Government Code and Cypher School at Bletchley Park as they race to break the codes used by Nazi Germany.
