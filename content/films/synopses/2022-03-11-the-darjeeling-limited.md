Three estranged brothers, played by Owen Wilson, Adrien Brody, and Jason Schwartzman, agree to meet in India a year after their father's funeral for what they call a healing trip aboard a luxury train, in this 2007 comedy-drama directed by Wes Anderson and co-written with Roman Coppola and Schwartzman. The trip quickly goes off course, complicated along the way by over-the-counter pain killers, Indian cough syrup, and pepper spray. Fox Searchlight Pictures released the film on 29 September 2007.
