Zakariya Mohammed writes and directs this 2018 Malayalam sports drama. When a local soccer club manager takes in an injured Nigerian player to recover at his home, the two form a bond despite the cultural and language differences between them. Soubin Shahir stars alongside Samuel Abiola Robinson in the title role. The film won five awards at the 2018 Kerala State Film Awards, including Best Screenplay.
