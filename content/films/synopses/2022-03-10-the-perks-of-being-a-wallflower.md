Stephen Chbosky writes and directs this adaptation of his own 1999 novel, set in Pittsburgh in 1991. Logan Lerman plays Charlie, an introverted high school freshman who forms a bond with seniors Sam and her stepbrother Patrick, played by Emma Watson and Ezra Miller, as he navigates undiagnosed post-traumatic stress alongside his first experiences of friendship and love. The film won the Independent Spirit Award for Best First Feature.
