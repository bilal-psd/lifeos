Wes Anderson directs this 2009 stop-motion film, adapted with Noah Baumbach from Roald Dahl's 1970 novel. George Clooney voices the title fox, who grows restless with domestic life and plans a spree of thefts against three local farmers, drawing his family into a manhunt led by the farmers. Meryl Streep, Jason Schwartzman, Bill Murray, and Willem Dafoe also voice characters. Anderson developed the project with Henry Selick starting in 2004 before it moved to 20th Century Fox.
