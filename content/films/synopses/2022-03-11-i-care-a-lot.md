J Blakeson writes and directs this film, which premiered at the Toronto International Film Festival in 2020 and reached streaming audiences in February 2021. A court-appointed legal guardian who defrauds elderly clients and seizes their assets takes on a new client who turns out to be connected to a dangerous mafia boss. Rosamund Pike stars alongside Peter Dinklage and Eiza Gonzalez. Pike won a Golden Globe for the role.
