The Wild Robot, written and directed by Chris Sanders and released in 2024, adapts Peter Brown's 2016 novel of the same name. After a shipwreck strands service robot Roz on an uninhabited island, she adapts to survive, builds relationships with the local wildlife, and becomes the adoptive mother of an orphaned goose. DreamWorks Animation used a watercolor visual style inspired by classic Disney animation and the work of Hayao Miyazaki, and Kris Bowers composed his first score for a fully animated feature.
