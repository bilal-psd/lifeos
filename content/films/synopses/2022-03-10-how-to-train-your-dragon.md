How to Train Your Dragon (2010) is set in the Viking village of Berk, where a teenage outcast named Hiccup is expected to kill a dragon to prove himself. Directed by Chris Sanders and Dean DeBlois for DreamWorks Animation, the film has Hiccup injure a rare dragon he later can't bring himself to finish off, instead naming it Toothless and secretly befriending it against his chieftain father's wishes. The story is based on Cressida Cowell's 2003 novel.
