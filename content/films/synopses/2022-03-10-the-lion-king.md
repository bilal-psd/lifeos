Roger Allers and Rob Minkoff direct this 1994 animated musical from Walt Disney Feature Animation. Young lion prince Simba, voiced by Jonathan Taylor Thomas and later Matthew Broderick, is exiled from his kingdom after his uncle Scar, voiced by Jeremy Irons, kills his father Mufasa, voiced by James Earl Jones, and takes the throne. Simba grows up away from home before deciding whether to return and confront Scar and reclaim his place as king.
