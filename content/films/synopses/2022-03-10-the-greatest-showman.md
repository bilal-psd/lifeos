Michael Gracey directs this musical drama, a heavily fictionalized account of P. T. Barnum, the showman who created the circus that grew into the Barnum & Bailey Circus. Hugh Jackman stars as Barnum, with Zac Efron in a supporting role, singing original songs written by Benj Pasek and Justin Paul. The film received mixed reviews from critics, but its song "This Is Me" won the Golden Globe for Best Original Song.
