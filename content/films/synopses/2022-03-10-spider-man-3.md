The final film in Sam Raimi's trilogy, released in 2007, picks up a year after Spider-Man 2 as Peter Parker prepares for a future with Mary Jane Watson. An alien organism alters his powers while he contends with the shape-shifting Sandman, played by Thomas Haden Church, his rival-turned-nemesis Eddie Brock, played by Topher Grace, and a vengeful Harry Osborn. Raimi wrote the screenplay with his brother Ivan and Alvin Sargent.
