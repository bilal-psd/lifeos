David Fincher directs this crime thriller, written by Andrew Kevin Walker, who drew on his own experience moving from the suburbs into New York City during a period of rising crime in the late 1980s. Morgan Freeman plays William Somerset, a nearly retired detective in an unnamed city, paired with new partner David Mills, played by Brad Pitt, to track a killer staging murders around the seven deadly sins.
