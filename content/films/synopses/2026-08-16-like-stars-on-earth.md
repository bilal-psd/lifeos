Aamir Khan directs and stars in this Hindi psychological drama, also known as Taare Zameen Par, his directorial debut. Darsheel Safary plays Ishaan Awasthi, an eight-year-old who notices the small wonders around him but struggles badly in class, prompting his parents to send him to boarding school. There, an art teacher played by Khan suspects Ishaan has dyslexia and works to help him overcome it.
