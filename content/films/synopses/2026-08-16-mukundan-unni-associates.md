Abhinav Sunder Nayak directs this 2022 Malayalam dark comedy crime film. Advocate Mukundan Unni is determined to achieve success, prosperity, and respect by any means necessary, and his ambition, fed by greed, comes at a price. Vineeth Sreenivasan stars alongside Suraj Venjaramoodu and Aarsha Chandini Baiju. It is the first film in Nayak's Success Trilogy, followed by Mollywood Times.
