Shawn Levy directs this 2024 film, the thirty-fourth in the Marvel Cinematic Universe and third in the Deadpool series. A listless Wade Wilson is pulled back into action when his home universe is threatened, and he reluctantly teams with a version of Wolverine from another universe to stop the Time Variance Authority from destroying it. Ryan Reynolds and Hugh Jackman star, joined by Emma Corrin. The film is the first R-rated entry in the Marvel Cinematic Universe.
