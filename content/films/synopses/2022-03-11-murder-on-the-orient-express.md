Kenneth Branagh directs and stars in this 2017 mystery, adapted by Michael Green from Agatha Christie's 1934 novel. Branagh plays detective Hercule Poirot, who investigates the murder of an American businessman aboard the Orient Express as it travels through 1930s Europe. The ensemble cast includes Penelope Cruz, Willem Dafoe, Judi Dench, Johnny Depp, Michelle Pfeiffer and Daisy Ridley among the train's passengers. It is the first film in a planned Poirot series.
