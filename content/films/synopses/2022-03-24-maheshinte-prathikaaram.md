Dileesh Pothan makes his directorial debut with this Malayalam film, based on an incident from a man's life in the village of Thuravoor. Fahadh Faasil plays Mahesh, a studio photographer whose involvement in an anonymous fight sets him on a path of revenge that forces him to reconsider his life. The film was a commercial success and won the National Film Award for Best Original Screenplay.
