Jeethu Joseph writes and directs this 2013 Malayalam-language crime thriller, the first film in the Drishyam series. Mohanlal plays Georgekutty, a family man whose daughter is secretly filmed by the son of a senior police officer, Geetha Prabhakar. When that son later goes missing, suspicion falls on Georgekutty and his family. Shot largely in Thodupuzha and released in December 2013, the film follows the family as they come under police investigation.
