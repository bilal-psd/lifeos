Nia DaCosta directs this 2023 film, the thirty-third in the Marvel Cinematic Universe, sequel to Captain Marvel and continuation of the series Ms. Marvel. When Carol Danvers investigates a wormhole linked to a Kree revolutionary, her powers become entangled with those of Kamala Khan and her estranged niece Monica Rambeau, and the three begin swapping places whenever they use their abilities. Brie Larson, Iman Vellani, and Teyonah Parris star.
