Harry Potter, Ron Weasley, and Hermione Granger return to Hogwarts for a second year in this 2002 sequel to Harry Potter and the Philosopher's Stone, directed by Chris Columbus from a Steve Kloves screenplay based on J.K. Rowling's novel. A message painted in blood announces that the Chamber of Secrets has been opened, and the three investigate as a monster petrifies students, helped along by a flying car and a house-elf's warning. Warner Bros. Pictures released the film on 15 November 2002.
