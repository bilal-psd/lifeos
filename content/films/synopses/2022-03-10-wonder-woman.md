Patty Jenkins directs this fourth film in the DC Extended Universe, starring Gal Gadot as Diana, an Amazon princess raised on the island of Themyscira. After American spy Steve Trevor, played by Chris Pine, washes ashore and tells her of the First World War, Diana leaves her home to confront what she believes is the work of Ares, the god of war. Robin Wright, Danny Huston, and Connie Nielsen appear in supporting roles.
