Jonathan Demme's 1991 film, adapted by Ted Tally from Thomas Harris's 1988 novel, sends FBI trainee Clarice Starling to interview Hannibal Lecter, an imprisoned psychiatrist convicted of murder and cannibalism. Her supervisor Jack Crawford wants Lecter's read on an active case involving a killer known as Buffalo Bill, and thinks Starling may draw him out where others have failed. Jodie Foster plays Starling, Anthony Hopkins plays Lecter.
