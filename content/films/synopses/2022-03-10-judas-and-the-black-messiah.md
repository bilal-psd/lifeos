FBI informant William O'Neal, played by LaKeith Stanfield, infiltrates the Illinois Black Panther Party on orders from Agent Mitchell and J. Edgar Hoover in this 2021 film directed by Shaka King. Set in late 1960s Chicago, it centers on the betrayal of party chairman Fred Hampton, played by Daniel Kaluuya, as O'Neal is pulled between the two sides. King wrote the screenplay with Will Berson; Jesse Plemons and Dominique Fishback round out the cast.
