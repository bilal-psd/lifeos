Bradley Cooper makes his directing debut with this 2018 musical drama, also writing and starring alongside Lady Gaga. Cooper plays Jackson Maine, an alcoholic musician who discovers Ally, played by Gaga, a singer close to giving up on her career, and draws her into the spotlight. As her career rises, their relationship strains under the weight of his addiction. It is the third remake of the original 1937 film, following versions from 1954 and 1976.
