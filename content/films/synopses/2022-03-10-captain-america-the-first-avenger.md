Joe Johnston directs this 2011 film, the fifth in the Marvel Cinematic Universe. During the Second World War, the frail Steve Rogers volunteers for an experimental program that transforms him into the super soldier Captain America, and he sets out to stop the Red Skull from using the Tesseract as an energy source for world domination. Chris Evans stars alongside Hugo Weaving, Hayley Atwell, and Tommy Lee Jones. Filming took place in London, Manchester, and Liverpool.
