Zack Snyder directs this 2009 film, adapted by David Hayter and Alex Tse from the comic book series by Dave Gibbons, published by DC Comics. Set in an alternate 1985 at the height of the Cold War, after a government crackdown has ended the era of costumed vigilantes, the story follows a group of mostly retired superheroes who investigate the murder of one of their own and uncover a conspiracy threatening humanity. Malin Åkerman and Billy Crudup star.
