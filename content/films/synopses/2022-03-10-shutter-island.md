Shutter Island, directed by Martin Scorsese and released in 2010, adapts Dennis Lehane's 2003 novel. Leonardo DiCaprio plays U.S. Marshal Edward Teddy Daniels, who travels with his partner Chuck to a psychiatric facility on a fictional island in Boston Harbor to investigate a missing patient, only for troubling visions to complicate the case. Development began after Phoenix Pictures acquired the rights directly from Lehane, and the finished film leans on a classical score built from composers including Gustav Mahler and György Ligeti.
