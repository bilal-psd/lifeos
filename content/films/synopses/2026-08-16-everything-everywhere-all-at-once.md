Daniel Kwan and Daniel Scheinert write and direct this 2022 film, blending surreal comedy, science fiction, fantasy, martial arts, and immigrant narrative. Michelle Yeoh plays Evelyn Quan Wang, a Chinese American immigrant who, in the middle of an IRS audit, discovers she must connect with versions of herself across parallel universes to stop a being bent on destroying the multiverse. Stephanie Hsu and Ke Huy Quan also star. Kwan and Scheinert began developing the project in 2010.
