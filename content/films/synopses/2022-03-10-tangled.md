Nathan Greno and Byron Howard direct this animated Disney musical loosely based on the German fairy tale of Rapunzel, a young woman with magical hair confined to a tower by her controlling mother. She strikes a bargain with Flynn Rider, a wanted thief, to leave the tower and see the floating lanterns on her birthday. The film combined computer animation with a hand-painted visual style.
