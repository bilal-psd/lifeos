Three cousins from Kerala, played by Dulquer Salmaan, Nivin Pauly, and Nazriya Nazim, move to Bangalore to chase their own ideas of what they want from life, in this 2014 Malayalam-language film written and directed by Anjali Menon. The film opened in 205 theaters on 30 May 2014, one of the widest releases for a Malayalam film at the time, and went on to earn an estimated 45 crore rupees, winning three Kerala State Film Awards including Best Actor.
