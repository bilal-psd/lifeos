Chris Buck and Jennifer Lee direct this 2013 animated musical from Walt Disney Animation Studios, loosely inspired by Hans Christian Andersen's 1844 tale The Snow Queen. Kristen Bell voices Anna, princess of Arendelle, who sets off with the iceman Kristoff, voiced by Jonathan Groff, and the snowman Olaf, voiced by Josh Gad, to find her estranged sister Elsa, voiced by Idina Menzel, after Elsa accidentally traps the kingdom in eternal winter. Lee wrote the screenplay.
