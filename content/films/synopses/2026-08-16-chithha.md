Chithha, directed by S. U. Arunkumar and released in 2023, stars Siddharth as Easwaran, a government employee whose niece Sundari goes missing. Wrongly suspected of kidnapping her himself, Easwaran learns a child abuser has taken her and makes it his mission to bring her home. Arunkumar developed the story after speaking with survivors of child abuse and NGOs, and the Tamil film won seven awards, including Best Film Tamil, at the Filmfare Awards South.
