Andy Muschietti directs this 2019 sequel to It, completing a two-part adaptation of Stephen King's 1986 novel. Set 27 years after the Losers' Club first defeated Pennywise, the story follows the group, now grown and scattered from their hometown of Derry, as a phone call reunites them to face the shape-shifting entity again. James McAvoy, Jessica Chastain, Bill Hader, Isaiah Mustafa, Jay Ryan, James Ransone, and Andy Bean play the adult Losers, with Bill Skarsgård returning as Pennywise.
