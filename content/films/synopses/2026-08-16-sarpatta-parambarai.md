Pa. Ranjith directs this 2021 Tamil-language sports drama, set in the 1970s and centered on the rivalry between two boxing clans in North Chennai, Idiyappa Parambarai and Sarpatta Parambarai. When Sarpatta Parambarai is challenged to a decisive match, the young laborer Kabilan must decide whether to fight for his clan despite his mother's disapproval and the dangerous politics surrounding the sport. Arya, John Kokken, and Shabeer Kallarakkal star. Filming was interrupted by the COVID-19 pandemic partway through, in February 2020.
