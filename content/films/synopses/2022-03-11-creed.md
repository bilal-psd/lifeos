Ryan Coogler directs and co-writes this 2015 sports drama, the seventh film in the Rocky franchise. Michael B. Jordan plays Adonis Creed, son of the late boxer Apollo Creed, who seeks out Rocky Balboa, played by Sylvester Stallone, to train him as a fighter. Rocky, once Apollo's rival and later his friend, takes on the role of mentor. Tessa Thompson and Phylicia Rashad round out the cast.
