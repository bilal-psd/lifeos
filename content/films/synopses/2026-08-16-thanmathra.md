Blessy writes and directs this 2005 Malayalam drama, based on Padmarajan's short story Orma. Ramesan Nair, a man with a loving wife and two children, begins forgetting things and growing disoriented, and is eventually diagnosed with Alzheimer's disease, a diagnosis that devastates his family. Mohanlal plays Nair, in a performance considered among his best. The film won five Kerala State Film Awards, including Best Film and Best Actor.
