Three Billboards Outside Ebbing, Missouri (2017) follows Mildred Hayes, played by Frances McDormand, who rents three roadside billboards criticizing the local police chief, Bill Willoughby, over the unsolved rape and murder of her daughter. Martin McDonagh wrote, directed and produced the film. The billboards provoke Willoughby's volatile deputy, Jason Dixon, played by Sam Rockwell, escalating tension between Mildred and the Ebbing police. Woody Harrelson plays Willoughby, with Abbie Cornish, John Hawkes and Peter Dinklage in supporting roles.
