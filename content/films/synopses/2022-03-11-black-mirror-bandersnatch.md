Charlie Brooker writes and David Slade directs this interactive installment of the Black Mirror anthology series, which lets viewers make choices for its central character. Set in 1984, it follows programmer Stefan Butler, played by Fionn Whitehead, as he adapts a fantasy gamebook into a video game while his grip on reality wavers. Released on Netflix in December 2018, Bandersnatch won two Primetime Emmy Awards.
