Aashiq Abu co-produces and directs this Malayalam medical thriller, dramatizing the 2018 Nipah virus outbreak in Kerala and the response of health workers and officials who worked to contain it. The film has a large ensemble cast including Kunchacko Boban, Indrajith Sukumaran, Tovino Thomas, and Parvathy Thiruvothu, and was later named among The Hindu's top 25 Malayalam films of the decade.
