Directed by Anthony and Joe Russo and written by Christopher Markus and Stephen McFeely, this 2018 film is the nineteenth in the Marvel Cinematic Universe. Thanos, seeking all six Infinity Stones, threatens to erase half of all life in the universe, and the Avengers join forces with the Guardians of the Galaxy to try to stop him. The script drew on Jim Starlin's 1991 comic The Infinity Gauntlet. The story continues directly into Avengers: Endgame the following year.
