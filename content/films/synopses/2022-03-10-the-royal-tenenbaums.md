Royal Tenenbaum and his wife Etheline, played by Gene Hackman and Anjelica Huston, raised three children who were all considered geniuses, in this 2001 film directed by Wes Anderson and co-written with Owen Wilson. Their early promise gave way to two decades of failure and betrayal, generally blamed on their father, and the film follows the family through a sudden, unplanned reunion one winter. Danny Glover and Bill Murray round out the cast, and the film is framed as an adaptation of a nonexistent novel.
