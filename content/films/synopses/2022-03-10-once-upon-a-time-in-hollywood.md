Quentin Tarantino writes and directs this film set in 1969 Los Angeles, starring Leonardo DiCaprio as Rick Dalton, a television actor known for westerns, and Brad Pitt as his longtime stunt double, Cliff Booth. Dalton lives next door to Sharon Tate, played by Margot Robbie, and her husband, director Roman Polanski. The film won two Academy Awards, including Best Supporting Actor for Pitt.
