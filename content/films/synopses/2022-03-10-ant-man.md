Peyton Reed's 2015 film is the twelfth entry in the Marvel Cinematic Universe. Scott Lang, newly released from prison, is recruited by scientist Hank Pym to wear a suit that shrinks its wearer while increasing strength, and to help keep the technology from falling into the hands of Pym's former protege, Darren Cross. Paul Rudd stars alongside Michael Douglas and Evangeline Lilly. The story turns on a heist rather than a citywide battle, keeping its scale deliberately small.
