A widow leaves her life in Nevada after the Great Recession takes everything from her and takes to the road across the American West living out of her van, in this drama written, produced, edited, and directed by Chloé Zhao. Frances McDormand stars, in an adaptation of Jessica Bruder's 2017 nonfiction book Nomadland: Surviving America in the Twenty-First Century. Real-life nomads appear as fictionalized versions of themselves. The film premiered at the Venice Film Festival on 11 September 2020, where it won the Golden Lion.
