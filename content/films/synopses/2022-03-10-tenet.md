Tenet (2020) follows a CIA officer, played by John David Washington and known only as the Protagonist, who is recruited into a secret organization investigating objects that move backward through time. Christopher Nolan wrote and directed the film, producing it with his wife Emma Thomas after spending more than five years developing the script. Robert Pattinson, Elizabeth Debicki, Dimple Kapadia, Michael Caine and Kenneth Branagh also star as the Protagonist traces the objects toward an attack originating in the future.
