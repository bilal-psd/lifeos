Toy Story 3 (2010) picks up as Andy prepares to leave for college and his toys, including Woody and Buzz Lightyear, are accidentally donated to the Sunnyside Daycare Center instead of being kept in the attic. Directed by Lee Unkrich from a script by Michael Arndt, the film follows the toys as they navigate daycare politics and decide where their loyalties lie before working to get back to Andy. It is the third installment, following Toy Story 2 in 1999.
