Harry Bradbeer directs this 2020 mystery, adapting the first novel in Nancy Springer's Enola Holmes Mysteries series through a screenplay by Jack Thorne. Millie Bobby Brown plays Enola, the teenage sister of the detective Sherlock Holmes, who travels to London searching for her missing mother and ends up using her own sleuthing skills to help a runaway lord while staying a step ahead of her famous brother. Henry Cavill and Helena Bonham Carter also star. Filming began in July 2019.
