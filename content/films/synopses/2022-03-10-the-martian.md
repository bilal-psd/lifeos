The Martian (2015) follows astronaut Mark Watney, played by Matt Damon, who is presumed dead and left behind on Mars after his crew is forced to evacuate during a storm. Ridley Scott directed the film, adapting Andy Weir's 2011 novel through a screenplay by Drew Goddard. Watney has to rely on his own ingenuity to survive with limited supplies while NASA, aided by a cast including Jessica Chastain, Chiwetel Ejiofor and Kristen Wiig, works out how to bring him home.
