Nick Cassavetes directs this 2004 film, based on Nicholas Sparks's 1996 novel, from a script by Jeremy Leven and Jan Sardi. An elderly man reads from a notebook to a fellow nursing home resident, recounting the story of a young couple separated by the Second World War who reunite years later after taking different paths. Ryan Gosling and Rachel McAdams play the couple in the 1940s scenes. The film became a sleeper box office hit.
