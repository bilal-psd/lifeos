Ruben Fleischer directs this 2018 film, the first installment in Sony's Spider-Man Universe. Tom Hardy plays Eddie Brock, a struggling journalist who becomes host to Venom, a violent alien symbiote, after his attempt at a career comeback goes wrong. Eddie must rely on his new abilities to protect the world from a shadowy organization hunting for a symbiote of its own. Michelle Williams and Riz Ahmed also star. Jeff Pinkner, Scott Rosenberg, and Kelly Marcel wrote the screenplay.
