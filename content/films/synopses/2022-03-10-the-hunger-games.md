Gary Ross directs this first film in the Hunger Games series, adapting Suzanne Collins's 2008 novel. In a future Panem, the Capitol forces each of twelve districts to send two young tributes into a televised fight to the death; when her younger sister is selected, Katniss Everdeen volunteers to go in her place. Jennifer Lawrence stars alongside Josh Hutcherson. The film grossed $695.2 million worldwide.
