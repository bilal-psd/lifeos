Chad Stahelski directs this action thriller from a script by Derek Kolstad, starring Keanu Reeves as John Wick, a retired hitman who comes out of retirement after gangsters kill the dog left to him by his late wife, his last connection to her. Michael Nyqvist, Alfie Allen, Ian McShane, Willem Dafoe, and John Leguizamo round out the cast. Stahelski and David Leitch, both veteran stunt coordinators, co-direct the film's action sequences together.
