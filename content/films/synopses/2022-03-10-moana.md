Moana (2016) is set in ancient Polynesia and follows Moana, the daughter of a village chief, who is chosen by the ocean to return a stolen relic to the goddess Te Fiti after a blight threatens her island. John Musker and Ron Clements directed the film for Walt Disney Animation Studios from a screenplay by Jared Bush. Moana sets out to find the demigod Maui, voiced by Dwayne Johnson, whose theft of the relic caused the blight, to enlist his help.
