Lovely little arthouse film. The cinematography and music totally elevates the film, and it was a pleasant surprise to know that they were done by the brothers Ranabir and Dhritiman Das. 

Now I do know there are people who would appreciate it more than I do. But as someone who generally disregards arthouse films are pretentious, this one had its moments.
